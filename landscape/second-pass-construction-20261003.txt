REUSE / MIGRATION / ABANDONMENT

Direct reuse candidates are small pure leaves and content: bounded presentation
tree/backends; supported syntax/validated edits; authored story assets; exact carry/
manifest byte checks; source/identity graph rendering after authorized data input.
Each needs a real consumer and current dependency/format/identity adaptation check.

Semantic migration: authored kind/instance/realm publication, collective choice,
source review/branch installation, provider acknowledgment, lease/continuation,
private allocation, budget reservation and archive/custody. Their useful contracts
survive but authority/law/evaluator/history/current privacy must be Mini's actual
ones. A raw source port or hash anchor does not accomplish migration.

Abandon unhelpful mechanisms when replacements connect consumers: app-private fake
ledgers, duplicated authority truth, unsafe heap mutation before revision, full-cell
observation, per-project async task twins, raw signing-seed exports, RAM-only storage
sold as durable market, universal mutable-heap union, settled-root-without-install,
operator identity masquerading as participant. Do not delete source/history or
remove aspirations because implementation is weaker than its claim. This is a
forward integration proposal; no files were removed by this research pass.
