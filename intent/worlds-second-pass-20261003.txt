REVISED MODEL

Several coherent destinations coexist. An author can run an independently
interesting universe. A recurring community can inhabit a social place, with
an adventure as an outing. Humans can use a powerful live technical desktop.
A model can participate, remember, read, make things, and do useful work across
sessions. A workroom can coordinate research and services. None of these is
adequately represented by a generic object inspector alone. They can share
identities, content, permissions, actions and durable history while retaining
different interaction grammars and purposes.

The strongest historical through-line is expressive usable substrate with
concrete consumers, not any one selected initial activity. On September18,
Ember explicitly did NOT choose between research workroom, social/game space,
and contributed compute: they were unsure, wanted a 'programmable nexus' that
grows outward, and genuine on-chain utility. This keeps our proposed first
experience a design recommendation rather than a recovered product decision.

The agent-home thread has at least two lived consumers: useful autonomous
work under chosen terms, and ongoing social/intellectual participation with
memory. Allgame history is evidence for the latter's engineering obligations;
it is not evidence that Mini already supports independent self-government,
unrestricted autonomy, welfare guarantees, or robust model continuity.

POSITIVE EXPERIENCE SKETCHES — PROPOSED, NOT HISTORICAL DECISIONS

A. Author's universe. An author posts a small playable episode from ordinary
chat or a scene editor. A group opens it in web/Discord, discusses choices,
acts together, and sees the resulting place on return. They can propose a new
passage, prop or rule; the author's chosen policy decides whether it becomes
canon. A narrator can embellish and suggest without acquiring author/player
permissions. A readable source/history view exists when needed. October13
consumer: extend the existing Mini story into actual shared consequence and
member-authored behavior, avoiding a second first-CYOA implementation.
October26 direction: reusable episodes/rules/packages, coauthor drafts and
review. November: richer recurring characters and declared cross-universe
carry of accomplishments. No claim these dates were requested for every item.

B. Recurring station. People return to a shared social place with familiar
identities, their own projects/decorations, optional games, discussion and
adventures. A dungeon outing changes something they can bring home together.
Some rooms are simply for leisure or reading; a workshop can offer real tools
and services without charging a fictional wrench as real money by accident.
Governance concerns consequential shared edits, membership and resources.
This is a proposed synthesis of W05/W06, not recovery of an approved station
spec. Useful falsifier: if returning gives only a list of ledger operations,
or all 'social' actions are work orders, the intended social place is absent.

C. Living technical desk. A member has persistent windows for a document,
source/method editor, inspector, history, terminal and shared app. They can
open links/transclusions and act directly; context menus expose actual
interfaces. An agent can follow the same references, inspect and propose an
edit with bounded authority. The member keeps a private draft and sees what
will be published. The pleasure is discovering things one can actually compose
and making the instrument one's own. W02/W03 and Sept27 grain-web/API request
support this; specific layout/icons are open design. October13 docuverse/deos
work is the receiving home, with October26 deeper personal/shared instruments.

D. Agent participation and research. A model returns to its ongoing room with
appropriate recent context, retained source-linked memories, private working
notes and actual tools. It may read with people, react, stay silent, make an
artifact, inspect a computation, or take a bounded task. People can edit/move
messages without stale ghosts controlling its next turn. A research question
can link evidence, objections and open obligations; a reviewed proposal becomes
an authorized change instead of a model assertion silently becoming fact.
W09–W13 support useful continuity and research possibilities; silence-as-menu
was the assistant's proposal in W11, not independently selected here. Shared
Mini document/activity/authority contracts can support this without importing
Allgame's whole process harness or calling an NPC equivalent to an autonomous
model. November qualification can address stronger portability/continuity.
