WHY OBJECTIVE BEND
An architectural argument to preserve across retirements and compactions
Primary-source scholar, 2026-10-03

This document preserves reasons, not an implementation status or an agent plan.
It distinguishes Ember's recorded direction, Faré's actual arguments, and our
construction choices. A returning peer should be able to disagree with a choice
without accidentally discarding the purpose it served. Contemporary source and
execution claims belong to the active language owner. The source citations below
refer to the preserved primary corpus; the October 3 census was read for the
boundary between that purpose and what has actually been established.

The experience comes before the evaluator

The intended system is a place in which people and programs can keep doing things:
inspect an ongoing computation, understand the objects and obligations involved,
change a tool, combine it with another person's contribution, keep the useful
history, and resume work without reconstructing its meaning from disconnected
files. A member should be able to author behavior as part of using the world.
An agent should have durable working context and explicitly granted means to act,
not only a prompt and an ephemeral process. A shared document, a programmable
instrument, an institution and a fictional place can inhabit the same semantic
infrastructure without being reduced to one interaction style.

There is direct human evidence for the breadth and for a correction to its imagery.
In the May 24 exchange, Ember rejected an executor/circuit unification project as
an adequate north star and described a Polis-like layer where models could have
a home and coordinate under their own terms. In the June 24 exchange, Ember
rejected the assistant's sentimental rendering and demanded technical, detailed,
Pharo/Smalltalk-like actuation and composition with spatial persistence. Read those
as compatible: continuity and agency are purposes; a particular home or pet
metaphor is not prescribed. Nor does the aspiration establish present autonomy,
security or model continuity. Exact read dialogue locators are recorded in [U1].

Faré's Houyhnhnm argument supplies an unusually useful way to judge this ambition.
The object of design is the entire interaction of users, development, execution,
storage and change. A small language inside a vast, informal support arrangement
is not thereby a simple computing system. Count the glue, recovery conventions,
manual schema work and developer intervention too. His appeal to live systems is
therefore not nostalgia for a particular image-based desktop; it is an argument
about where meaning and responsibility reside. Our inference is that Mini becoming
Dregg needs an authored world language whose concepts continue to mean something
in its editor, running world, debugger, persistence and implementation strategies.
A method registry behind a UI cannot by itself supply that continuity. [P1]

Why the unit of construction is a partial specification

A useful contribution is often deliberately incomplete. A review policy describes
how to combine judgments but needs a provider of evidence. A notebook extension
adds one behavior while relying on a storage interface. A geometry family adds a
constructor and needs the rest of the family to agree about its recursive types.
Requiring each contribution to be a closed executable before it can be passed,
stored, inspected or combined turns the useful intermediate object into something
outside the language. People then reconstruct its requirements and combination
rules with build scripts, registries or conventions.

Faré instead reconstructs OO as modularity and incrementality made available as
ordinary in-language computation. A specification can be a value before it has a
complete target. An extension expresses a change in terms of both its inherited
starting point and the eventual context in which it will live. Programs can make,
parameterize, return and compose such extensions. A factory can capture a reusable
collaborator, not merely emit a fixed selector-to-code table. The important test is
whether a member can write that factory and use its result inside the language;
a host API capable of installing predefined methods is not equivalent evidence.
This is the central argument of EOOMI and poof, developed in LTUO. [P2]

Incrementality here first means constructing a new specification from existing
ones without restating everything they contain. It does not automatically mean
an incremental compiler, memoization of all calls, efficient dependency tracking,
or live migration. Those can be enabled by preserving the relevant structure,
but each needs its own semantics. Conflating these meanings makes it easy to claim
the whole design after implementing only a cache or a sequence of overrides.

Why self, super, mix and fix belong together

Suppose one extension computes a bill-of-parts count from self.parts, while later
extensions contribute more parts. The original count must see the final collection,
including contributions that were unknown when it was written. If each stage sees
only the prefix already constructed, the count is stale. Conversely, an override
that intentionally wraps the inherited result needs that particular inherited
meaning, not another lookup through final self. These two relationships explain
self and super. They are not interchangeable aliases for current object state.
Faré's bill-of-parts example makes their interaction concrete. [P3]

An extension is therefore open in two directions: the eventual context and the
inherited target. Using our self-first convention, its initial semantic shape is
C → V → W. C is the final context, V the inherited target, W the provided result.
The book writes the arguments super-first. Composition supplies the same final
context to both extensions while feeding the earlier result into the later one:

  compose parent child self base = child self (parent self base)

Fix then ties the recursive context when a construction is ready to close. A
homogeneous C=V=W algebra is a useful special case. It cannot define the general
operation: a function target may be extended using a richer record context, or an
extension may change representation. For fixed C, heterogeneous composition is a
category; the endomorphisms form a monoid. This distinction affected the actual
language discussion because a neat homogeneous theorem could otherwise conceal
lost source expressiveness. [P4]

Even C → V → W is only the first typing approximation. A reusable extension must
work with future self and inherited TYPES satisfying its requirements, including
fields it did not know about when compiled. Value-dependent families over one
fixed C do not establish this future-type polymorphism. The pressure comes from
ordinary recursive families, associated types and binary methods, not an exotic
optional corner. Faré sketches row/subtyping and recursively constrained types;
he does not deliver a complete checked type theory for us to adopt. We must choose
an explicit tractable checking discipline and show which constructions it admits.
Treating today's record examples as that theorem would lose the reason for the
generalization. [P5]

Why lazy meaning became a substantive correction

Open recursion does not logically mandate one evaluation strategy for every
language. Strict systems can support guarded recursive methods with explicit
indirections. But our strongest reading aims to preserve arbitrary computational
targets, incomplete recursive constructions and usable parts of partially defined
objects. Eagerly demanding the whole construction can destroy that meaning before
any wanted component is available. This is why laziness is a semantic choice here,
not merely an optimization for recursive calls. [P6]

Two small examples separate the choices. keep(7, fix(identity)) returns 7 if keep
does not demand its second argument; a strict call evaluates the divergent argument
before keep can run. Likewise {good=7, bad=self.bad}.good can yield 7 even though
bad is cyclic. Suspending the whole record is insufficient if forcing the record
then forces every field. A third example selects one expensive field twice: both
pure call-by-name and call-by-need can yield the same result, but only the intended
shared thunk implementation avoids repeating that field's initialization. This is
per allocated thunk identity, not a promise to memoize every equal function call.
The three independently authored Objective Bend source consumers preserve these
questions concretely; their expectations are source observations, not borrowed
backend premises. [E1]

Fix also cannot mean merely “choose a solution to x=f(x).” Every value satisfies
x=identity(x), yet operational fix(identity) produces no weak-head result. The
algebraic equation is useful after choosing a semantics; its witnesses do not
prove successful construction. Likewise finite approximants need to agree through
appropriate observations, not return literally identical closure syntax: a closure
containing the recursive fix and one containing a finite unrolling can be related
without being the same syntax. Pure call-by-name and call-by-need have the same
convergence behavior in the ordinary unbounded pure setting; their sharing/cost
difference must not be falsely reported as a general termination difference.

This motivates the new independent lazy source meaning and an explicit graph
machine, rather than declaring the retained BendTT call-by-value machine to have
become lazy because it skips erased arguments. Suspended/evaluating/cached cells,
update frames and graph identity are plausible representations. They remain
replaceable if another implementation preserves the observations, sharing and
resource contract actually promised. Blackhole diagnostics, capacity suspension,
errors, strict primitives and effect forcing still need deliberate boundaries.

Why incomplete reflection is more than an object inspector

If inspecting a specification first forces its incomplete target, reflection fails
at exactly the point where someone needs it to finish the construction. Faré's
prototype treatment keeps the specification available alongside a lazy target;
recursive self must also retain access to the specification. Wrapping only the
already-constructed result is too late. This gives a practical reason to retain
specification identity and structure: users and programs can extend, inspect and
repair a contribution before all its requirements are discharged. [P7]

Our proposed organization keeps computational fix general and constructs reflective
prototypes explicitly, tying the wrapper inside the recursion. The exact tuple,
record or closure representation is not sacred. Neither is unrestricted syntax
inspection automatically required: the language must say whether reflection exposes
names and parent structure, extension functions, code/captures, abstract semantics,
or some combination. Every exposed observer constrains valid optimizations.
A descriptor listing names can help inspection but is not a substitute for the
first-class computation that the specification performs.

Inheritance order is also part of meaning, not housekeeping. Noncommuting replace
and wrap operations produce different results in different orders. Shared DAG
ancestry is different from explicitly composing the same extension twice. Suffix
layout demands a contiguous tail, stronger than preserving ancestor subsequences.
C4 is a serious candidate for expressing these requirements, not an article of
faith. Our primary reading found that the book's bare-DAG shape-determinism wording
is stronger than its implementation can support: equivalent partial orders with
different ordered presentations can select different linearizations. The defensible
invariance is under renaming of the ordered presentation, including its tie-break
and suffix data. Preserving this correction matters more than preserving the name
of the current algorithm. [P8]

Interfaces, laws and authority answer different questions

An incomplete contribution needs a way to say what it requires, and composition
needs a way to establish that the resulting collaboration meets those requirements.
A callable shape is often insufficient: a client may depend on an invariant, an
ordering law or a relation between several operations. This motivates source-level
interfaces and behavioral promises with actual evidence. Final-self assumptions
must eventually be discharged for the closed object; a collection of per-method
proofs under unproved assumptions is not a proof of the whole collaboration.

It does not follow that every extension must preserve every law ever attached to
an ancestor. Faré explicitly allows constructions that are not monotonic subtype
refinements, and distinguishes inheritance from orthogonal protocols supplied after
the fact. Our construction should separate unrestricted building from adoption as
a particular compatible interface. A scoped protocol environment can allow new
collaborations without requiring everyone to edit a common ancestor. Whether this
uses dictionaries, rows, witnesses or another representation remains a design
choice; silently changing the selected implementation is a governance concern too.
[P5, P9]

Authority is another question: who may invoke an effect, publish code, adopt an
upgrade or disclose a result now? Behavioral conformance does not grant authority;
permission to install code does not repair a false behavioral promise. Ownership
is also distinct. Pure reusable closures can capture other reusable closures, but
an immutable outer record cannot make a captured one-shot obligation duplicable.
A copyable resource address is not evidence of possession. Across suspension,
sharing and recovery, the system must preserve whichever ownership and custody
contract it has chosen. “Consumed exactly once on every run” is too strong for a
run that remains suspended forever; nonduplication, conserved obligations and
justified terminal discharge are safety properties, with liveness stated separately.
These are our world-language obligations, not consequences of the word object.

Why Bend is a starting calculus, not a ceiling

Bend supplies a valuable concrete calculus, explicit quantities, checking rules and
metatheory to examine. It also supplies a useful retained executable fragment.
But the October 3 human correction is explicit: Objective Bend is its own language;
we need not preserve Bend's type system precisely. The earlier Data-only reusable
capture, acyclic-linking and total-call restrictions were properties of one proposed
embedding. When those restrictions obstruct the intended first-class construction,
we should change or qualify the embedding, not redefine the destination. [U2]

The Fable review was valuable because it asked whether the large collection of
artifacts actually supplied that meaning. The first council proposal made existing
restrictions do too much conceptual work: first-order Data targets, clocked strict
CPS, retained ancestor laws and serialized residuals appeared to solve questions
that they merely narrowed. The later exchange retracted Data-only source captures,
affinity-by-Data and safe-point-by-serialization. Preserve both the criticism and
the constructive possibility: defunctionalization can represent closures, and a
total first-order interpreter can implement partial lazy programs if it represents
suspensions, environments and effects faithfully. Tables and CPS are not inherently
disqualified. Treating their current restricted instance as the full source language
is what failed. [E2]

General recursion cannot silently acquire the original total calculus's consistency
or termination theorem. A total proof fragment and a partial computational fragment
need a sound boundary. This does not prohibit proofs ABOUT partial programs:
contextual equivalence, simulation, safety and partial correctness can be stated
in total logic over program syntax and semantics. A successful finite trace proves
termination of one computation; it is not the only legitimate law about recursion.
Whether the final discipline uses judgment modes, Comp types, guarded fragments or
a combination remains open. The essential condition is that running arbitrary
recursive computation does not become a way to manufacture arbitrary logical
proofs or make type conversion diverge without an explicit account.

The larger reflection argument: changing how a running thing is implemented

Prototype reflection is one level of the story. Houyhnhnm computing asks for a
further capability: changing the implementation beneath a running computation
without requiring its author to rewrite the computation around every persistence,
debugging, distribution or optimization strategy. Otherwise every improvement
creates another incompatible software stack and another manual migration. The
point of a semantic tower is to make the relation between abstract behavior and
concrete execution available to the system itself. [P10]

First-Class Implementations and Climbing give this ambition a stronger formulation
than a fashionable “meta-object protocol.” Concrete states include intermediate
steps that may have no immediate abstract counterpart. An implementation therefore
needs designated observable states and an interpretation of transitions/effects,
not only a map of values. To recover a computation interrupted at an arbitrary
reachable concrete state, observability must construct a safe route to an abstractly
meaningful state, with bounds excluding unbounded work or blocking intervention.
Soundness, completeness, progress and observability are distinct requirements;
one forward simulation can leave the machine stuttering forever or omit wanted
source behavior. [P11]

This is why proof shape matters. A relation that already assumes the desired
source evaluation has not explained execution. A theorem that returns a projection
of its premise has not connected independently defined meanings. Conversely,
requiring a decreasing administrative rank on terminal self-loops makes a proposed
representation impossible to inhabit. Classify running, terminal, suspended and
refused states; connect initialization and final observations; prove progress on
the transitions where progress is required. The independent source is valuable
because it gives these claims something external to their own implementation to
answer to. Axiom counts and a compiled module are useful checks, not substitutes.

Continuity forces persistence and evolution into the language argument

If a resident's work is to survive, persisting a result or a state record is not
enough. The computation includes its control state, environments, captured code
revisions, heap sharing and outstanding interactions. If a person's tool is to
evolve, keeping that computation forever pinned to the first program version is
not enough either. Houyhnhnm chapters 4 and 5 connect these pressures: preserve
code with data, represent upgrades as coherent changes, retain the distinction
between renaming and deleting/recreating, and track incomplete transformations.
These are proposals for making change intelligible inside the system, not claims
that distributed upgrade becomes trivial. [P10, P12]

A concrete counterexample guards the boundary. A residual continuation tag with
{amount=100} remains perfectly serializable after a new version interprets amount
as dollars rather than cents. It is not thereby the same continuing computation.
A typed field converter alone does not relate the code that will consume it.
Likewise a checkpoint that omits an uncertain outgoing action can replay it after
recovery. Correct migration must relate continuation meaning, code/data coherence,
effects already performed or pending, and ownership/fencing. A declared safe-point
policy can defer upgrade until these relations are simple; a stronger migration
may transfer complete recovery state without first resolving every uncertainty.
Neither should be claimed from successful decoding alone. [P11, P13]

Stable object identity, current revision, the code an in-flight invocation uses,
and permission to adopt new behavior should remain distinguishable. Pinning old
invocations while new ones adopt a revision is a coherent initial policy. Live
frame transformation is a stronger policy with stronger obligations. Faré's LTUO
code-upgrade discussion leaves such policy questions open; our multiuser world
must choose them explicitly. Forked exploratory change must also confine actual
external effects, not merely show a different history cursor. [P12, P14]

Why multiple execution routes belong here, without becoming one vague promise

The human direction calls for native, oblivious, proof-producing and specialized
execution, with FHE among the broader routes. The semantic-tower argument explains
how this can be one language project instead of several unrelated evaluators: a
program's specified meaning can remain stable while its implementation strategy
changes. It does not make those strategies interchangeable without qualification.
Privacy depends on permitted observations, access patterns, scheduling, faults and
release; proof soundness depends on the actual compilation and cryptographic chain;
resource limits depend on an explicit cost model. A recursion-bounce count is not
a bound on arbitrarily expensive work inside a single terminating arm. Extensional
mix laws do not establish identical allocation or public charges. [U2, P10, P11]

The strongest coherent construction therefore preserves a rich source meaning and
makes backend coverage explicit. A specialized circuit may implement one sealed
fragment extremely well. It need not pretend to execute arbitrary dynamic reflection
to be useful. Nor may the existence of that fragment silently remove reflection
from the language. The remaining work is real semantics, typing, executable
refinement and governed interaction, not merely connecting components whose names
look compatible. The October 3 census explicitly preserves that distinction.

What must survive changes of representation

The durable commitment is to in-language, reusable, partially specified construction;
final-self collaboration with distinguishable inherited meaning; an explicit account
of recursive/lazy observations; inspectable and evolvable ongoing computation; and
interfaces, evidence, ownership and authority that retain their different meanings.
The computing system should automate the repeated preservation work around these
concepts rather than require every member to recreate it manually.

Tuple layout, syntax, the particular row discipline, C4, thunk states, continuation
encoding, Comp-versus-judgment modes, a chosen migration policy and current backends
can change. Some will need to change as counterexamples and usable programs teach
us more. There is no fully proved Objective Bend metatheory hidden in the corpus:
Faré supplies powerful constructions, examples and research conjectures, while the
current implementation still owes substantive correspondence and system obligations.
Preserving that distinction keeps both ambition and criticism available. A returning
peer should be free to find a better mechanism while still answering the original
question: can people understand, compose, continue and change the computing they
are living with, from within a system whose behavior remains accountable?
