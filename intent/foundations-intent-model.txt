THE MOST CONSEQUENTIAL CHANGED UNDERSTANDING

1. The early sovereignty requirement is a topology and user experience, not just
   a cryptographic adjective. On May 23 Ember asks that cells live entirely offline
   among relevant partners, with federation involvement when needed, and endorses
   this direction. On May 24 they explicitly reject two browser tabs sharing one
   runtime and reject full-history transfer as the substitute. Each tab should run
   its own local JavaScript and still coordinate through human communication. [F01]
   A hosted shared world is a valuable later mode, but cannot by itself fulfill this
   earlier property. A document/room interface should have an independent local
   executor mode, portable authority/evidence and a boundary settlement contract.

2. Authority is generative as well as restrictive. May 30 Ember explicitly corrects
   "monotonic narrowings forever": sealer/unsealer amplification, powerboxes and
   third-party introductions are needed dynamical classes. This correction later
   recurs beneath a June 19 agent claim that amplification is impossible. The actual
   Spec/Authority source contains authorized Amplify, Mint, Endow and consented
   self-Origin, alongside restrictive attenuation/revocation. [F04] Its Origin
   header records the practical failure of the old empty-graph fixed point: no
   first grant could be created. This is more than terminology. A platform that
   knows only attenuation may be very safe and unable to begin anyone's life.

3. Canonical cameras do not automatically provide numeric conservation. The May 29
   agent sold sum preservation and authority confinement as one FPU law; the human
   chose canonical cameras and requested recursive caveats about other cells.
   The checked source defines Fpu as compatibility preservation, gives Nat an
   always-True validity predicate, and proves EVERY update of an always-valid
   algebra frame preserving. Thus Nat 0 -> 100 is FPU there. [F03] This does not
   refute separate balance-conservation theorems elsewhere. It does refute using
   that abstraction identity as a substitute for explicit accounting laws. Preserve
   the resource richness and separately enforce actual supply/balance invariants.

4. Native resource meaning need not mean native currency. TokenAutonomy's concrete
   theorem forbids a lossless homomorphic retract from nontrivial idempotent
   membership into cancellative numeric amounts. [F08] Its "own token" is an
   autonomous NON-NUMERIC resource algebra. The theorem does not say users must pay
   in DREGG, nor prohibit representing objects on a foreign chain with extra state.
   Keep account funding, conserved resource claims, membership/permissions and
   speculative tokenomics distinct. July 25's skeptical staking/burn exploration
   is not a theorem fixing a burn-only monetary constitution. [F09]

5. Stronger deployed semantics was an early correction to the agent's plan, not a
   late taste change. May 29's agent proposes a small Lean golden oracle and says
   differential testing will certify Rust. Same-day human prompts explicitly
   redefine metatheory as Dregg2 itself and want Rust to host compiled Lean for
   actual semantics, including consensus/finality and privacy. [F02] Later consent
   to independent Rust diversity and leanc in the trusted base does not restore
   the original false certification claim. Distinguish a trusted compiler from
   unchecked second authorship of semantics or constraints.

6. October traffic privacy is an unresolved seam brought back into implementation
   scope, not an unrelated new feature. July's actual handoff/resurrection documents
   already acknowledge storage placement, PoR audits and recovery fetches reveal
   metadata; caps/encryption do not settle that. October 1 explicitly asks for a
   coupled dissemination/agreement/constant-rate construction, then October 2 makes
   some traffic privacy and agreement an alpha condition. [F11,F12] Those are stronger
   than an observer-view theorem over a pure projected value. They must consume the
   physical sender/receiver/host/recovery paths, without forcing private execution
   to be FHE everywhere or treating pasted Gemini prose as architecture.

THE FOUNDATION EMBER ACTUALLY SELECTED

The repeated destination is a powerful, understandable semantic computer: rich
objects, programs, authority, retained causal histories and private cooperation,
inhabitable by humans and agents. Formal methods are supposed to expose wrong
models and make implementation less fragmented. They are not an alternate product
consisting of soundness models that no living consumer invokes.

May 29 is a particularly instructive compressed decision cycle. Human #4317 lists
linguistic abstraction, proofs, reasonable efficiency and future Rust integration
as advantages, and explicitly fears detached formalization and integration cost.
The agent recommends a small semantic spec and claims difftesting certifies Rust.
Human #4341 accepts Lean-core-first, independent authority/ordering choices with
"diagonal default," and freezing the old frontend for a rebuild. The same day #5306
says metatheory IS Dregg2, expects approximately all semantics in Lean with raw
crypto/bots/web as exceptions; #5456 specifies compiled Lean hosted by Rust for
actual semantics of actual things. [F02]

Therefore "core first" must not be quoted as endorsement of the agent's entire
oracle architecture. The detailed follow-up narrows what the acceptance means.
Likewise "freeze current and rebuild later" is a staging decision from an early
rebuild, not perpetual permission to leave every consumer frozen.

May 30 #6497 rejects factoring from variant names instead of real definitions.
The rejected collapse hid different relationships: bearer delegation chains with
facet attenuation, recipient-bound handoff certificates, stealth point relations,
OneOf alternation, and token authorization bound to actual request facts. [F04]
The desired abstraction is allowed to become smaller only after these semantic
relationships survive. A universal "verify statement" seam can be useful while
remaining insufficient to describe who attested WHAT to WHOM at WHICH root/time.

June 2 #10444 asks whether treating delegation as one action per cap scales for
moving cap-containing data structures, invoking seL4 guarded CNodes as comparison.
The surrounding agent admits neither flat per-cap actions nor scoped forest edges
solve the container problem. [F05] This is an underread tension for flat Mini
hyperedges: flattening transactions can simplify coordination without answering
portable aggregate authority. A manifest/container cap could designate a bounded
collection with explicit lookup, attenuation and revocation semantics. That is a
new design proposal, not a command to revive Mina-style call forests.

AUTHORITY, SOVEREIGNTY AND PROVIDERS MUST NOT COLLAPSE INTO ONE ROOT

The June 7 question whether financial flavor was "mind-poison" is real exploration,
not an instruction to erase finance. The assistant immediately proposes demoting
about sixteen effects and calls money a demo. Ember corrects this: effects were
pulled into the kernel deliberately to prove their guarantees, including private
payment for distributed resources, while individuals should compute in their own
cells without coordinating beyond their partners. Hoisting may become appropriate,
but a kernel-minimality audit is not the immediate destination. [F06]

The assurance frontier is consequently a contract frontier, not a vocabulary
frontier. A userspace escrow is acceptable if its guarantees really match or exceed
the kernel route; an ordinary mutable map with methods called escrow does not
inherit that assurance. Mini may express fewer generic transition primitives and
more typed resource/contract families, provided ordinary users can still buy/rent,
create, meter, delegate, recover and settle actual resources privately.

A cell's owner, a community's current governance, a physical host's resource policy,
and a participating model's instruction authority should be separately expressible.
September 18 explicitly selects resources deliberately governing and potentially
locking their own management. [F13] That choice supports meaningful self-government
and removes an implicit owner repair override. It does not imply a host has no
control over machine availability, or that revoking a content read capability
makes already-disclosed bytes disappear. "Refuses operator" needs the exact operator
and exact accepted action boundary named.

July 6's human suspicion that flaw-free proof attempts were not trustworthy prompts
adversarial rereview. [F07] Actual GovernedDynamics stores a holds proof as a field;
governed_holds simply projects it. PolisDynamics consumes independent policy/shield
safety premises. This packages a conditional invariant usefully, but does not by
itself establish political non-domination or remove physical operator control.
The strongest preserved meaning is: an enforcement mechanism refuses particular
unauthorized/unsafe transitions despite an adversarial controller. It then needs
continued capacity, custody, recovery and exit capabilities to be useful to a resident.

TIME/HISTORY IS COMPUTATIONAL POWER, NOT ONLY EVIDENCE

June 19 asks for fresh execution from historical points with willing parties,
virtualizing a past, nested isolation and useful lossy reconciliation to main.
These are human exploratory questions. The assistant responds that almost all of
this is existing composition and offers two small protocol additions. [F10]

The source reveals a concrete mismatch beneath the branch protocol's confinement
story. BranchStitchSession::fork creates a WHOLE World::fork, then separately mints
a culled UmemBranch as its stitch baseline. World::fork clones the entire ledger,
factory registry, executor signing key and every receipt-chain head. The branch's
world() exposes that whole ledger; turn/drive run through that cloned executor.
The offstage-cell test checks absence only from baseline.cells. Thus the comment
claiming offstage cells are structurally absent from every branch exceeds this
implementation. open() also constructs the session without a joint-consent argument.
Its stitch returns a verdict and does not apply the result to main. [F10]

This is a useful simulator: wakes are fresh and persistence is None, so the source
explicitly separates main-memory state and disk/observer publication. It is not a
checked proof of namespace/capability/physical-effect confinement. No external-effect
leak was run or asserted here. Grain::fork uses a different mechanism: it clears the
inherited c-list and confers only parent-held selected targets under a separate lease.
That is a concrete authority-culling design to reuse, still requiring effect/permission
semantics review. SettlementSoundness's named generic theorem assumes BindsLiveAuthority
and directly applies that premise; this read does not verify a deployed settlement weld.
Reconciliation must bind current authority and causal/conflict/supply rules; external
actions already completed cannot be undone by scrubbing a history cursor.

July 8 #45 asks how disseminating umems/sovereign state will preserve enough
houyhnhnmness. The adjacent response makes a valuable distinction: public root,
actual contents needed to resume, integrity vs hiding, read authority, metadata.
SOVEREIGN-RESURRECTION specifies encrypted shards, tip anchors, cap-scoped sync,
policy-selected checkpoint/retention and explicit branch resurrection. [F11]
This is a richer survival target than a SQLite reopen success at one root. A useful
portable cell must carry or obtain the dependency/code/content/authority closure
needed to resume, while separately choosing which physical effects remain available.
This closure may be delegated and rented; it should not force global storage of
every private object's contents. Identity, state version and checkpoint root deserve
separate representations so a portable object does not become a new social subject
every time it changes a field.

GENERATION, PERFORMANCE AND THE 10% REQUEST

July 2 Dragon's Orb is motivated by an explicit source-available/relicensing boundary,
world-class performance and assurance, feeding a seL4-based future. This is not proof
that copying old network source into an AGPL repository is authorized. [F14]
July 3 Ember accepts executing with leanc in the trusted base while compiler work
continues, rejects replacing complete Orb with depth-over-breadth and testing-as-
correctness. July 8 again says custom compilation is vital for scalability/longevity
but not necessary to run the cloud immediately. The same surrounding report records
a specific representation bottleneck: cons-list processing dominated while socket
zero-copy work disappeared beneath it. [F14] Preserve the destination and measure
actual representations; do not infer either "Lean cannot be fast" or "proofs ensure
speed" from these episodes.

The actual EmitLean example generates instantiated seam theorems from shared lemmas,
including recycling count, op origin and invariant preservation. [F14] It is a good
mechanism for deleting repeated recipes. The actual demonstration uses unitComponent for both inputs; H1 and h2c lanes call
the identical reactor step, and the Route seam means submission provenance, not HTTP
routing correctness. Its accepted compiler syntax does not by itself prove the whole
HTTP RFC, configure every existing middleware or generate optimized machine code.
Research and codegen outputs need a precise consumer map.

July 28 #105 explicitly rejects thinking of 260 Pickles steps as handwritten work:
this should be compiler/translator output. [F15] August 6's initial Mini request
extends that to the whole proof-native semantic computer, explicitly kernel,
hyperdreggmedia and DeOS, at most ten percent of old LOC. August 9 asks subsumption
across DrEX/FHEgg/Bazaar, DeOS, token authorization, grains, agents, cloud/control
planes with good performance. [F16] This is a programmatic breadth-preservation
request, not a claim that 10% is achieved or a quota to delete useful functionality.

The dated August subsumption artifacts provide a good distinction among semantic,
admission, cryptographic, deployment and benchmark maturity. Their history also
warns against declaring "missing work is no longer another kernel design" too
strongly: later sparse-model/OO/traffic work legitimately reopened foundations.
A generated source boundary can still hide a wrong semantic statement; a proof can
still rely on an unrealized handler or assumption. Source byte identity answers
one question, while meaning, cryptography and physical receiving answer others.

CURRENT MINI CONSUMER IMPLICATIONS — PROPOSED

A small set of reusable contracts should enable a large set of real experiences:
- Resource births/factories with inspectable authority endowment, exact installed
  code/policy identity, child limits and independent lifecycle invariants.
- Human/agent invocation through the same typed request and authority semantics,
  with delegation containing collections and explicit self/parent/powerbox growth.
- Local sovereign operation plus negotiated joint scopes and boundary settlement;
  shared hosting is a mode. Agreement operates where conflicting shared resources
  require it, not as mandatory public coordination for every private keystroke.
- History cursor/snapshot/suspend/branch/stitch as distinct operations, explicit
  physical-effect portals and dependency closure for resumption on another device.
- Private paid service/resource paths whose public release, executor observation,
  provider custody and traffic observation are independently specified.
- Generated effect/controller/codec/proof/dispatch surfaces reducing repeated work,
  with a canonical semantic model retaining materialization and performance refinements.

The checked current Mini files show useful nuclei, not complete closure: PrivacyProfile
explicitly keeps public observation, hiding claim, execution visibility and metadata
independent; ResourceCost models exact semantic charges separately from external I/O;
ResourceBirthAuthority installs a batch checked against the original pre-authority;
CanonicalResourceBookInvariant proves supported-account and balance-hidden registration
properties. [F17] They are specific better replacements for overgeneral slogans.
No build or actual deployment was examined in this pass, so these source reads do
not certify the current public system or the October13 bar.

The unresolved design questions and targeted next readings are retained in

artifact; they are not historical assertions or a new whole-kernel implementation
