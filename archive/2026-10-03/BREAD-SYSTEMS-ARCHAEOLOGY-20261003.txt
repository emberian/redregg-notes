JUDGMENT
Bread's underused prize is a portable, governed HOME for computation and shared artifacts: rent an agent/body, share distinct facets, retain acknowledged history, sleep/restart/fork it, recover bytes from untrusted providers, and observe exactly what one's current authority permits. That product bundle deserves a place beside Mini's OO and collective-fiction work. Bread's implementation is uneven: warm exact history and several confinement/custody components are stronger than a shallow inventory suggests; distributed storage markets, sparse observations and general private execution are much weaker than their names suggest. Port contracts, receiving tests and finite mechanisms selectively. Importing its parallel state/authority stacks or its cost model would make Mini more complicated.

FIVE CONCRETE MINI ADAPTATIONS

A. A certified session revision/index shared by receiving, receipt lookup, views and history.
Consumer: NativeHostSession, DurableReceiverIO.Loaded, NativeHostReplay, NativeObservationController and authored document history. Keep exact List-spec/current authority and first occurrence/enumeration semantics; execute indexed representation incrementally behind equality witnesses. Cache only revision-owned canonical opening data, never reader-specific authority. Structural writes/ratchet must invalidate correct derived surfaces; complete-cell replacement cannot import stale digests.
Cost/deps: representation/proof join across roughly4 native modules plus existing PresenceIndex/LinkIndex pattern; no new circuit/crypto. Dominant remaining terms are whole-history prefix materialization and full per-entry wire view; reducing them requires a second coherent representation/root decision. Opening cache work is already active; don't assign a duplicate port.
Cheapest falsifier: repeated same-history narrow view; then changed incoming clone, hidden-only write, birth, refusal and restart at every intermediate boundary, comparing exact bytes/root. October13 relevance: usable community world latency/continuity; later relevance: scalable histories/docuverse.

B. Source-authorized continuation custody plus participant-held acknowledged checkpoint.
Consumer: application lifecycle/provider/Hermes output/agent home and private recovery continuation. Native record binds exact source/package/input/candidate/generation, outcome/outbox and custody base; archive-first append yields exact typed successor or typed uncertain/poisoned state. Pin acknowledged history under user's actual native signing authority. Recovery and retention follow outstanding obligations, not current arbitrary TTL. Separate acknowledged prefix integrity, physical action provenance and private output release.
Cost/deps: a common recovery record/physical IO refinement plus service integration; secret export/rekey and private holder-generation proofs are substantial independent work. Avoid duplicating current service custody work. Cheapest falsifier: durable archive advances but anchor/reply is lost; restart surfaces exactly one candidate, wrong acknowledgement refuses, new attempt cannot re-dispatch old uncertain physical action; later completion/root advances preserve old delivery evidence.
October13 relevance: one real interruption/reply/restart user journey; October26/November relevance: portable contributed hosting/migration, private recovery lifetimes. These are priorities, not dated promises.

C. Reserved resource service that cannot oversell irreversible work.
Consumer: provider purse/RunComputeBudget plus private evaluator, agreement/repair/outbox and transport. Exact admitted cost vector reserves compute, preprocessing, persistent bytes, ingress/egress cover and recovery work. Retain native Book/current authority; source decides release/compensation. Independent local spending uses disjoint rights whose total fits actual capacity, or explicitly labels bounded speculative harm before irreversible work. A recovery job's minimum service persists independently of original caller.
Cost/deps: scheduler/physical metering/atomic reservation installer, chosen capacity classes and lifecycle census. No magical resource-camera port provides service fairness. Cheapest falsifier: two honest workers consume simultaneous maximum reservations, one peer floods low-cost jobs, masks need refill, origin goes offline; existing recovery still gets service without minting capacity/refunding spent work. October13 relevance: credible variable multiuser/private capacity; later: participant-operated economy.

D. Authenticated encrypted-artifact repair outside the single live host.
Consumer: durable Store backup/checkpoint, package and document SOURCE archives, later private ciphertext/output escrow. Manifest+position binding+exact bytes/source anchor from native admission; per-home candidate policy; grouped fixed-size erasure coding, retained chunks/proofs rather than whole re-encode per GET; bounded parallel timeout-aware retrieval and explicit repair generation. Choose failure domains and authority separately from bond ranking.
Cost/deps: physical durable chunk store/network fetch, manifest native binding, repair obligations and encrypted export. Bread codec/test reuse is realistic; deployed malicious/PQ custody is not obtained from the component. Cheapest falsifier: wrong shard position, duplicate shard, incompatible manifest, first host unavailable, accepted archive bytes missing after ledger commit, insufficient surviving independent providers. October13 role: actual restore qualification; October26/November role: decentralized hosting/large archives.

E. One causal transport/recovery envelope with stable semantic IDs and fair retries under privacy.

New send nonce is separate from stable action/nullifier, encrypted/padded on wire; reverse dependency index retains only missing work. Bounded queues use explicit fair cursors/priority reservation, not global oldest1024 seen hashes per topic. Fixed public schedule/capacity classes and admitted leakage are part of private transport profile; sender/subject authority does not become peer-address or successful-send authority.
Cost/deps: transport controller and chosen active privacy profile; no need to transplant Bread gossip/CM engine. Cheapest falsifier: freeze candidate state, lose first identical request/response, overload unrelated topic, restart with orphan dependencies, delayed old cert/custody obligation; completion remains exact and hidden participant leakage stays within selected game. October13 relevance: traffic privacy/agreement joined recovery; later: cross-home collaboration.
