JUDGMENT

The meaningful objective is a usable, extensible, private Mini community operating
environment. People should inhabit the same durable world, author and connect
knowledge, create useful kinds/behaviors, use hosted apps and resident Hermes,
and continue safely through retries, authority changes and operational failures.
Privacy and agreement must govern those real interactions. A two-person fixture,
an EtherCalc export chain, a working-set circuit, or an agreement simulator is
valuable evidence toward that objective; none is its replacement.

Current six construction lanes attack genuine blockers. Their largest coverage
gap is member-facing programmability and docuverse/deos depth. A kernel owner
fixing observation performance cannot also silently become the sole owner of
full OO, all document workflows, inspectors and deployment. Add explicit owners
or explicit substantial assignments for those outcomes. Do not declare the
objective achieved when the current fixes and first protocol tranches converge.

The recovered record supports three distinct kinds of commitment:
1. Traffic privacy and some consensus/agreement are an explicit October 13 alpha
   condition. Shipping that alpha without them contradicts a direct instruction.
2. Complete prototypes of full OO, stronger private execution, traffic privacy
   and distributed agreement are explicitly requested construction work. They
   must not be moved back into documentation-only remaining work.
3. Complete docuverse and substantial deos are explicit October 13 ambitions,
   with no recovered agreed exhaustive feature definition. Preserve them and
   operationalize them visibly; neither shrink them to passed floor tests nor
   claim every historical desktop/cryptographic ambition is a deadline mandate.

PROPOSED OPERATIONAL OBJECTIVE — MY RECOMMENDATION, NOT A RECOVERED QUOTE

Deliver a deployable and usable Mini alpha community world by October13: variable
members can join with their own authority and budgets, inhabit shared/private
places, author and navigate a substantial docuverse, define and reuse composed
object behavior, use member-owned hosted apps and resident Hermes through the
same durable contracts, and retain accountable work through crash/retry/restore.
Implement and join confidential computation, traffic privacy and distributed
agreement prototypes to real world operations, with exact supported guarantees,
fault/availability/leakage assumptions and measured costs. Completion requires
working native member experiences and a pinned integrated candidate, not separate
protocol demos or documentation-only designs. Full OO/private execution/privacy/
agreement stay construction destinations even where a particular alpha profile
has a bounded lifetime, population, capacity or fault model.

Recommended success scenarios (all consume one common semantics/candidate;
protocol fixtures may first refute mechanisms independently):

1. ARRIVE AND BELONG. An independently provisioned member enters Mini, sees what
   places/actions are available under their actual authority, joins a shared
   space, creates useful work, leaves and rejoins. Vary population and overlap:
   2,5,20 are test inputs, not capacities. Members have separate budgets/keys;
   parallel rooms/apps/residents do not use globally fixed member identities.

2. AUTHOR AND CONNECT KNOWLEDGE. Members create/edit/read documents in shell and
   browser, link and transclude live/pinned/range references, annotate/quote,
   search/follow and inspect history/current law. Another member with narrower
   authority gets a meaningful authorized view or private locked/refused result;
   an old hit/source reference never grants new access. Concurrent/stale edits
   retain drafts and exact outcomes. Protected content, key acceptance/revocation
   and source-authorized disclosure join this ordinary workflow. Useful views
   and inspectors matter; a command list alone does not deliver substantial deos.

3. EXTEND THE WORLD FROM WITHIN. Ordinary members author authenticated prototypes,
   compose real partial behavior using final self and super, create reusable
   instances and inspect definitions/laws/effects. A derived poll/workroom/board
   supplies actual behavior used by scenario2 or4. Prototype revision leaves old
   instances' pinned meanings intact and permits new instances to adopt new
   behavior. An inherited restriction refuses a real forbidden method effect.
   Demonstrate at least two useful views/actions over the same object and exact
   retry/accounting. A numeric ROM method table is a prerequisite, not full OO.

4. APPS AND HERMES LIVE THERE. A member starts/shares a real browser app; its
   output becomes an authorized document/source reference; an addressed resident
   reads it, performs useful work under delegated authority/budget, writes a
   summary/reply to the same world. Multiple authors, queue cancellation, running
   recovery and ownership changes work. Lost reply/SIGKILL/restart recovers exact
   already started work without second provider request/charge. Fake providers
   are fine for crash tests; actual useful model operation and BYOK are separate
   receiving evidence. No new paid calls under current instruction.

5. PRIVATE DISTRIBUTED ACTIONS. The member-authored method performs hidden reads,
   meaningful predicates, an atomic effect/message and private return across
   relevant governed domains. The faithful evaluator, custody, agreement engine
   and transport consume the same candidate/authority/result contracts. Delayed
   evidence, contested decisions, audience change, a failed domain and restart
   preserve exclusive outcome, exact installation and retained recovery. Traffic
   traces cover discovery/read/status/retry/repair as well as payload send.
   Confidential evaluation must qualify the actual chosen fault model; neither
   encryption alone nor a plaintext toy earns that claim. Source anonymity and
   endpoint probing leakage are distinguished. Whole-path proofs/refinements and
   adversarial executions substantiate the stated guarantees.

6. OPERATE A REAL SERVICE. Fresh source-pinned install, normal SYSTEM-managed
   restart, checkpoint/restore with app+resident data, client continuity and
   rollback checks, variable member/app uid allocation and bounded fair service
   all run. Record usable full-action latency and history-growth measurements;
   retain <=5s write/<=60s reopen at1000 as existing targets, add doc/app/resident
   full-action budgets from actual experience. Portable artifacts support Pug's
   deployment without recreating private fixtures. Mainnet entry must use a real
   supplied address/environment and source-owned quote/credit accounting before
   advertised paid public alpha; synthetic rail does not qualify it.
