ROOT JUDGMENT
The architecture is worth pursuing, and is more concrete after this exercise.
It retains Mini's full object semantics, Bread joint-turn/causal foundations,
private computation and traffic privacy ambition. It is not yet a closed proved
cryptographic protocol or a measured private Mini implementation. Agreement among
models is not evidence of either. The next work is precise construction/proof and
cost closure, not another general literature catalogue.

CURRENT RECOMMENDED STRUCTURE
One Mini invocation/effect/result relation; operation-specific joint identity and
complete authority/resource dependencies; independently governed domains. Use
local agreement for genuinely local choices, robust private computation for
confidential evaluation, and a joint certificate rule over necessary domains.
Retain the architecture report's Generic Simplex local-control choice as the
concrete baseline; the room did not establish a new faster universal winner.
MPC input agreement is not duplicated by a second public intake log. An explicit
completion/generation choice stays until the stronger private-output composition
proves it redundant. Bread CM remains a serious comparator with its identified
ChainExtends and epoch-closure obligations, not an already-qualified substitute.

REFINEMENT 1 — A REQUIRED PARTICIPANT VOTES LAST
Prefer binding a designated final signer d* into the exact joint candidate,
defaulting to the existing mandatory origin/nullifier domain. Prove d* is required;
do not introduce an unrelated coordinator quorum. Other required domains finalize
immutable YES or NO. d* stays undecided until it can durably retain all other YES
and required body/custody evidence, revalidate/reserve its own projection, and
atomically finalize its YES. Before that, its authoritative NO aborts the candidate.
The underlying joint law remains all required YES => Commit; any required NO =>
Abort. A delayed non-origin YES cannot override the final signer's NO.

Tradeoff, not dominance: a live last signer can abort while another domain remains
silent. Symmetric early YES cannot abort once all still-live domains already said
YES. Conversely, an early origin YES can permit later commitment after the origin
goes offline, where origin-last would block. Post-Commit installation and release
still depend on required services. Polling cannot manufacture a missing decision.
Do not market last-YES as universally nonblocking or finite-retention atomicity.

REFINEMENT 2 — RECOVERY IS A PRIVATE DURABLE JOB
Retain a source-authorized recovery continuation containing the secret participant
mask, exact candidate/generation, evidence-access rights, phase and origin fence.
It has reserved service and survives the caller/attempt. Public certificate
availability alone does not let anyone recover a hidden decision. Responsibility
includes log, installer, successor custody and repair, not merely ballot service.

REFINEMENT 3 — AUTHORITY HAS EXPLICIT COMMIT POINTS
Reservation grants exactly the later install obligation the current law permits;
it is not an old PreparedInvocation bypassing Mini's current-authority check.
Release admission separately authorizes one exact projection under then-current
policy. Physical packet delivery is not the point at which learned information
can be revoked. Do not default to unilateral YES expiry, compensating an arbitrary
committed method, or stealing an irrevocable reservation through wound/wait.

PRIVACY FINDING
Packet observers and authorized probing callers have different observations.
Padded encrypted reads can hide timing/contents from the first while plaintext
Pending/staleness or follow-on actions disclose dependency membership to the
second. A chosen partition can strengthen that distinction. The privacy claim
must specify allowed output/failure leakage and prove the exact observation game.
Correlated Pending is not proof of aliasing; distinct resources can share a joint
operation. Neither added public manifests nor default failure leakage was approved
as a shortcut. Fixed fanout and mixes still provide different association privacy.
An arbitrary relay is not a qualified active/PQ anonymous transport construction.

COST CORRECTION — SUPERSEDES THE EARLIER HARD 737/S WORDING
The cited >100*n field-element comparison has not been established as a measured
complete healthy steady-state coefficient or a universal bound for the selected
implementation. B/(w*alpha*n) is a sensitivity model. With B=9MiB/s,w32,n4,
alpha100 yields737.28/s, alpha20 yields3686.4/s, before other terms. These are
scenarios, not a proved Mini capacity ceiling. Online latency, preprocessing refill,
packing and total/per-link budgets must be counted separately.

POSITIVE FEASIBILITY IDEA
A secret bounded working set can reduce full-memory scans without publishing
method/address/miss traces. The proposed fixed-phase cache keeps a secret PC and
fetches/scatters real-or-dummy records on the same public schedule. For the given
A64,M256,W16,D4 illustration, mux-only work is53,248 versus262,144 naive selections
(about4.92x fewer); D8 gives139,264 (about1.88x). This is not measured speedup;
authentication, predicates, equality, dispatch, overflow and faithful completeness
must be included. Public capacity-class selection must already be allowed leakage.

WHAT THE ROOM REJECTED

fault model, replaced grant-or-NO with wait-on-conflict, or confused custody-holder
masks with audience release. Kimi/GLM revised or withdrew these after cross-review.
Their broader lower-privacy product preferences were not adopted. Two-holder
unavailability is a valid test of an UNQUALIFIED adapter, not evidence that a
stipulated qualified holder set may ignore its access-structure proof obligation.
