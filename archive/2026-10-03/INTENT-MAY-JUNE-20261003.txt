WHAT THE EARLY PROMPTS ACTUALLY ASK FOR

The broad ambition is a persistent, distributed, capability-secure environment in which humans and models can build, cooperate, use real resources and maintain a life of work. Authorization, privacy, proof systems and agreement support that experience. They are not the whole product. The May record repeatedly asks whether the ingredients cohere, then demands a runtime and applications that actually run. June makes the experience more concrete: live reflective images, desktop actuation and composition, replay/branch/stitch, social transports, a web-forward developer OS, and hosting/resources accessible to autonomous agents.

The strongest original articulation is May 24, claude:130628b0-ab87-41b8-8116-66d0de87ec49 @1434. Ember rejects the assistant's standard Cairo-style single-definition executor/AIR roadmap as an inadequate north star. They describe language runtime technology + object capabilities + Mina designs contributing to a Greg Egan-like Polis layer deployable that year, models with or without robots having “a home,” and AI-native coordination/cooperation/value building “autonomously and with liberty and freedom under their own terms.” The surrounding exchange matters: this is an expansion/correction of an architectural endpoint, not generic sci-fi encouragement. The adjacent references are ~/dev/svenvs, ~/elide/persvati, ./cell, CellProgram and CapTP. This is an explicit desired capability/experience, with deploy-this-year an aspiration stated at that date, not a verified feasibility result.

RECURRING CONSTRAINTS, PRECISELY BOUNDED

Coherence and usefulness: asks what is load-bearing/lying, prefers shared definitions/refinements over copied variants, rejects fragmentation into thousands of files. Apps/userspace and assurance should develop together, because a formally elegant WRONG/useless model commits the project to bad semantics (June3 5a0f6a2e @12711). Compiler/FFI/runtime consumers must consume what proofs describe.

Ownership and breadth: repeated rejection of awaiting permission on normal progress, closed-pillar declarations, tiny next-bite/handoff rituals and private future decisions agents already can investigate. This is not permission to overwrite another lane or ignore resource limits. May25 130628b0 @7505 requires distinct remote checkouts; May15 svenvs transcript limits huge HOL jobs to dedicated persvati due local RAM consumption; June6 @667 corrects a disk-image request misread as permission for a more expensive cloud instance.

Assurance: narrow semantic tests over real composition, not structural/grep-only “proof.” May25 130628b0 @11307 explicitly rejects structural tests that don't test anything. June19 rejects byte-identity ritual detached from meaning. Does not mean no tests; later demands broad behavioral/perf suites and workloads.

Communication: useful exciting explainers/marketing and accessible build instructions matter, with technical internals foregrounded only where users can act on them. “Honest framing” is useful description after work, never a replacement for implementing a known path.
