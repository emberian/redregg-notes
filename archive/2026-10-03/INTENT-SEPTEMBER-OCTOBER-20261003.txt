MAIN INTERPRETATION

The intended world is a programmable nexus people and agents can inhabit and extend:
social spaces, knowledge and project rooms, hyperdocuments, stories/games, useful
computation, and an eventual distributed operating system. Hosted agents and SPK apps
are useful inhabitants and entrances. They should help people build and use the
world rather than define its architectural boundary. Identity, governing laws,
authority, persistence, references, computation, transport, privacy and agreement
must compose through the actual kernel and native clients. The surrounding suite
also contains research instruments and mechanism experiments, not one homogeneous
product backlog.

There is no recovered requirement to build all that in one release. There is strong
repeated direction to keep the destination intact, make substantial improvements
through the stack, and choose demonstrable milestones that grow into it. Selecting
a small first scenario is permitted; quietly replacing the world with that scenario
is what Ember repeatedly corrects.

8. WHAT A HISTORICAL REPLAY SHOULD PRESERVE AND REOPEN

A proposed counterfactual replay can use three ledgers rather than one blended story:
(a) human requests/choices at the time; (b) later evidence and failures actually learned;
(c) new design proposals informed by both. Example: the September GitWeb narrowing
was genuinely an attempt to get an integrated journey working. Its later failure
teaches that generic construction/authority/hosting contracts should organize the
platform. It does not retroactively mean the initial SPK experiment was pointless.

High-value choicepoints to replay explicitly:
- Research iceberg → legible programmable nexus: ee15d4e6[443], 01a07270[179],
  01a0b0ed[357,761]. Preserve multiple social/creative/research uses.
- Grant/current-law and management self-lock decisions: 01a0b0ed[4862,5836].
- Foreground disconnect reversal and explicit soft mode: [6655→6659→6734→6796].

  data-model representation diagnosis is evidence, not the original premise.

- Nock adoption then reopen language choice: 0c315aee[2145,2180→4758,4770].
- Semantics before full STARK assurances: 01a0b0ed[6380], later soundness/
  autostarking questions 0c315aee[3313,3365].
- Private transport/agreement as coupled architecture and alpha condition:
  0c315aee[4504,4515], 01a0fa81[6864,6996,7479,7481].
- Variable multiuser platform and rebuild deployment model: 01a0fae5[7880].

My recommendation for future strategy: make a small set of reusable world-native
contracts produce several substantial experiences. A member-authored research room
with transclusion/annotations, a governed social/story space with composed behavior,
and an app/agent collaboration can exercise the same constructors, authority,
references, budgets, receiving and recovery paths. Private computation and agreement
should join those consumers visibly. Contributed hosting and DREGG-funded service
capacity can then extend the nexus without defining all computation as numeric jobs.
This is proposed synthesis, not a recovered new release mandate.

Actual unresolved preferences/private facts remain: exact first public activity;
homelab inventory/custody/availability; live payment receiving and backup recipients;
provider stake withdrawal/release details; exact anonymity/fault/latency/cost profiles;
which research limitations the next prototype deliberately accepts. Engineering
backlog decisions marked "deferred to Ember" are not standing barriers after
Oct2 cloud [289] delegates analysis and decision-making. A whole new kernel or
wholesale rewrite is not automatically authorized by an interest in historical replay.
