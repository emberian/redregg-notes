Do NOT equate the runtime's started marker with joint YES: started can mean uncertain controller/provider dispatch. The native lifecycle must retain separate submission, admission, irrevocable reservation, business installation and release facts.

Status is an authorized observation, not that fact itself. resident_requests.rs:146–210 and room_resident.rs:239–265 owe bounded typed notices and persist notice operation IDs before payment. Unaffordable/backlogged notices may be skipped; uncertain payment/write is looked up, never replaced. Therefore an absent completed notice does not mean unfinished work. The canonical status/recovery API must read the retained native receipt/phase; the room notice remains a projection. Current 'queued: N ahead' text explicitly discloses queue occupancy to its audience. Private profiles must authorize that leakage or replace it with an allowed coarse status; encrypting packets does not hide decoded status from that audience.
