JUDGMENT

The missing product lineage is substantial: authors running universes, audiences
collectively choosing and modifying worlds, players carrying identities and
accomplishments between adventures, and people/agents inhabiting an ongoing
social home. Ember explicitly names SS13 station as the ambition and a dungeon
as an away mission. Treating this as a generic multiplayer chat or a colorful
inspector example would lose the point. The platform's research workrooms,
hyperdocuments, institutions, games and resident agents are meant to coexist.

There is significant reusable construction, but not a finished station hidden
behind a build flag. Bread contains real narrative compilers, collective voting,
world/game modules, persistent sessions, cross-surface offerings, authored/remixed
universes and progression handoffs. It also contains independent app executors,
host-managed admission paths, fixed demo electorates and partially joined proof
paths. Mini already has kernel-governed sealed stories and a retained native
journey, which changes the best construction strategy: extend its existing
authoring/law/room path, borrowing Bread's content, contracts and hostile cases.
Do not port a private Bread executor into Mini and call that unification.

SOURCE MAP: EXPERIENCES, REAL CONSUMERS AND LIMITS

1. Authored narrative, choice and law.

~/dev/spween is a Rust narrative DSL with lexer/parser/AST/runtime and WASM
playground. Scene contains metadata and passages; choices carry conditions,
effects and navigation. World state is deliberately external behind
EffectHandler. This is reusable authoring syntax, not a multiplayer platform.
Read README and docs; Bread docs/deos/SPWEEN-ON-DREGG.md captures the original
integration design written for Kanzokax. Its future-tense claims are design.

Bread spween-dregg/src/lib.rs and compiler/world/collective/verify modules bind
scenes to actual WorldCell/EmbeddedExecutor/CellProgram turns. Choice gating,
certified decisions, receipt anchoring, replay and native durable checkpoint
custody are actual exported APIs. Source consumers include dungeon-on-dregg,
ugc-dregg, wasm story bindings and collective frontends. A stranger's replay is
O(history), distinct from a succinct history proof.

demo/stories/the-commons.scene and the-drowned-library.scene are actual authored
fiction assets. demo author/forge/coauthor/party/vault/region surfaces and browser
drivers preserve richer UX references. demo/real-dungeon-service exposes
/validate and /author then authored-session moves and verification against
real WorldCell. Rich .dungeon format covers NPC conversation, keys/flags,
combat/spells/light/consumables/status. Crucial mismatch: dungeon-on-dregg/src/
dsl/compile.rs:684–734 explicitly refuses constructs not lowered (spells, HP
combat and others); parser expressiveness is not executor compiler coverage.
Port supported constructs explicitly, fail on unsupported ones, expand general
law/compiler semantics as needed rather than dropping content behavior.

2. Collective choice is both story choice and institution, but not truth.

collective-choice/src/lib.rs uses actual ballot/poll cells, WriteOnce ballots,
monotone tallies, quorum admission, cap attenuation and weighted casting. It
is consumed by narrative branch selection and governance. It answers which
authorized choice a group made. It does not establish that a proposition is
true, a narrator honest, a model's prose canon, or a poll fair under every
electorate/economic regime. Preserve those distinctions when designing social
institutions; the ResearchRegime work is another relevant source for a later
governance scout.

demo/dungeon-service/src/coauthor_api.rs:1–95 and :776 onward is a concrete
collective world-editor: propose typed AddRoom/AddExit/PlaceItem/SetObjective;
ballot/quorum certifies winner; shared-draft::Draft::dispose re-renders and
re-validates, refusing/rolling back a breaking voted edit. It retains edit
dispositions/history. Actual limits: five fixed demo names, derived demo keys,
in-process draft; it does not implement production durable/custodial authoring.
shared-draft is a useful typed data/validator pattern, not finished collaborative
prose editing or a general canonical-world authority protocol.

dreggnet-web/src/crowd_round.rs and overlay.rs add live crowd ingestion,
transparent OBS overlay/SSE and timer close into a real world turn. make_app
mounts it under OVERLAY_LIVE_WORLD; default is tally-only, configured live mode
is demo Keep. Current source improves old public-name secrets to OS-entropy
platform custody; viewer-held signing remains absent here. Weighted votes
replicate shaped seats, cost O(sum shaped weights) under mutex despite weighted
primitive existing below. Dynamic persistent electorate and async multi-group
merge remain gaps. Good spectator/community experience inspiration; not a
ready Mini transport/privacy/agreement substitute.
