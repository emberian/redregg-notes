# nanodregg against the field: the nine judgments collated (real 2026-10-24)

Nine whole-file readers (Opus), each asked for a holistic judgment of its layer against the bleeding edge (HOLISTIC-BRIEF.md). ~3,700 lines of judgment; no edits, no builds. This is their consensus and their disagreements, in one place.

## 1. What nano IS, in the field's terms (where nine readers agree)

A deterministic single-writer state machine whose transition function (`World.admit`) is also its specification; four instances of one Merkle trie; members check the writer by RE-EXECUTION of the log, not by agreement (PeerReview 2007; SUNDR 2004; SPORC 2010; Certificate Transparency for receipts); versioned pins with BEHIND/AHEAD finality as optimistic concurrency (Kung–Robinson 1981; Sui/Fabric); capabilities as state (KeyKOS, SPKI, macaroons); value conservation with floors (EUTXO, XRPL trust lines); collision escapes over explicit inputs (Rogaway's "formalizing human ignorance", 2006). The language layer is a ledger language, not a mainstream PL (Cardano validators; Move with the opposite choice on value; Calvin's signed read set) over a CEK machine that IS the consensus semantics, with fuel whose bound is a theorem (the CakeML adequacy approach). The Rust is a sans-I/O effect handler (algebraic effects / Interaction Trees) under a Lean decider. The codec is a verified parser/serializer with canonicity proved BOTH ways (level with EverParse/Narcissus; ahead of deterministic CBOR, protobuf, SSZ), with content-addressed structural tags (Unison/Avro). The host is a redo-only write-ahead log with group commit and MAC-chained frames (Schneier–Kelsey), post-fsyncgate (Rebello et al. 2020). The resident is a reconciler over an interaction-tree effect model under a rely-guarantee socket model, with a KERI-style pre-rotating key custody and an object-capability key broker whose policy is a Lean decider.

## 2. What is genuinely NEW (each named by exactly one reader; none disputed)

- Laws indexed by the act they judge, including typed law REPLACEMENT judged by both the old and the new law (LANG).
- Refusals that carry the missing pin coordinates so a client can discover its read set (LANG).
- The writer periodically reopening its own Store in process and refusing if the result differs: continuous validation of the recovery path (HOST: "I know of no other system that does it").
- A theorem that swapping two same-typed cases leaves the structural tag unchanged, paired with the vector table that catches it (CODEC: "workshop-paper size").
- Consent before signature proved as a theorem about the signing path: a member signs only what its own copy admits (PROTOCOL).
- The gates' ability to prove they can fail, as standing checks (GATES: "I know of no verified project doing this").
- A keybroker whose policy is a Lean decider (RUST: "ahead of any production example I know").

Judged IDIOSYNCRATIC, not novel: the live holes ("guarded dynamic dispatch with de Kleer-style justification checks under CMTT vocabulary": LANG; "closest to Hazel's typed holes and truth-maintenance systems, no name in the literature": LANGPROOFS: the two readers disagree on whether this is a contribution); the per-window refusal counts (a rate limiter as a ledger invariant); the armed-rotation refinement of pre-rotation.

## 3. The ONE verdict all nine reached independently

**The base is first-rate; the trust method is a hand-curated catalogue of ~6,000 theorems audited by plants, and the field moved past that with seL4, CompCert, IronFleet and Iris.** Trust in those systems comes from ONE small reviewed statement (a semantics-preservation theorem; a chain of refinements ending at a short spec) with everything beneath it as untrusted scaffolding the checker covers. nano has one genuine refinement theorem (`Host ⊑ Kernel`, `host_run_audits`) but it refines to the 36,789-line kernel, which is its own spec, and it is proved against a follower nobody runs. So the trusted base is all 6,305 statements plus every definition they name; the census, the certificate and the declaration diff defend a boundary never drawn; and yesterday's three worst defects (receive before pin-compare; the decls gate blind to a definition's body; the escrow judge ignoring the legs) are all "a statement that is not the one the system needed", which no plant can catch. GATES: "the most honest instrument panel I've seen, pointed at 6,305 statements because the ten actually claimed were never written down."

**The plants, judged:** sound mutation analysis of specifications (DeMillo 1978; Beer et al. 1997; Kupferman–Vardi vacuity), real and under-practised, correct at what it measures (a statement is not trivial), and unable to say a statement is RIGHT. Keep them as vacuity checks. Stop treating "a plant reds a named theorem" as evidence of correctness and shaping proofs to produce it. Stop writing properties in docstrings with no theorem behind them: "every defect I found yesterday sat under such a sentence" (PROTOCOL).

## 4. The laws, judged

Load-bearing and correct: 2 (Lean decides; the proved function is the deployed one), 3 (canonicity: the fuse's one-θ is free), 6, 7 (refusals by name), 12 (the size ratchet), and law 4's derived BOUNDS. Aspirational (stated, not what the code does): 1 (twins everywhere: three re-derivers, two checkpoints, closed/live Program twins, three Expr serializers, six hex helpers), 4's lock sentence (the lock moved to the key handle in HOST-64 and the law was not updated), 7 in Lang (free-text refusals checked by grep), 10 (the decisions files are landing journals with status lines that lie; no spec beside them). Wrong-headed: 8 (a def/proof file split that leaves specification props nowhere to live; a build workaround that should be a gate); 11's carve-out (45 scripted rows "are the spec", so there is nothing to prove refinement against); law 2 READ as "the Rust needs no spec" (so Rust tests check Rust against the author's copy of Lean); second-agent vacuity reads used as a substitute for a spec.

## 5. The cuts, judged

Right: Kernel↔Lang through `Receive.Program` ("excellent"); Lang↔Protocol through `ReceiptCheck`; the Kernel/Receive cut (but ordered backwards at the read-set seam). Wrong or missing: the FOLLOWER is split across Host, Protocol and Kernel so no layer owns it as one object and the theorem about it does not exist (HOST, PROTOCOL, RUST all name this); the attempt state machine is split across the Lean/Rust boundary and that is where the worst Rust defect lives; `NanoBridge` makes Lang import Hosting; the kernel writes its own hash inputs and the codec proves its bytes equal to them (two sources); Lang lacks a core/std/theory split; no crash-semantic Store interface that proofs and the simulator share; storage has no owner (nothing but grants can be retired).

## 6. Single writer + receipts + no agreement engine

Eight of nine: the right call for this system, not a deferral (the P6 design memo's verdict stands). The deferral hiding INSIDE it, named by HOST and PROTOCOL: **fork consistency**. Within one World members never compare heads, so the Host can show two members different histories indefinitely without detection; SUNDR solved this with one server in 2004. Cross-World heads are unsigned and posted by grantees, with forks seen only at equal step counts.

## 7. The one structural change, per layer (each priced by its reader)

| layer | the change | cost |
|---|---|---|
| GATES | a `Spec/` layer: an abstract model (objects, balances, grants as a delegation tree, receipts) + the 5–10 statements the project claims + `pin_X : Spec.X` theorems the checker verifies; any meaning change under a claim becomes a build error at any depth | 1–2 months model + refinement; days for the pins |
| KERNEL | check every pin against the World BEFORE `receive` (makes KERNEL-54, "AHEAD stale is not final" and the consent story true together) | ~1 week (FIX-KERNEL is on it) |
| LANG | one loader, one setup, one install check per package; delete the closed twins | ~1 week (FIX-LANG is on it) |
| LANGPROOFS | a `MethodSpec` tier beside the generators + ONE generic "a run refines its spec" theorem + ONE generic Turn lift; the run-sound theorems become instances | ~2 months |
| PROTOCOL | keep each command's state INSIDE follow, typed by phase; the client holds a handle: constant-size continuation, a full page fits one frame, step 4 settles the call the argv planned | ~1 week (+ weeks for frame sizes as part of the type) |
| HOST | ONE re-deriver + a writer–follower agreement theorem over the real page bytes | weeks (FIX-HOST is on it) |
| HOSTING | follow's journal records the list of calls it absorbed; provenance stated against that list (kills the free fallback case; makes the end-to-end Bell statement statable) | 1–2 weeks |
| CODEC | every codec-vectors row checked three ways (kernel, compiled Lean, emitted Rust); hand-written Rust framing becomes emitted layouts | weeks |
| RUST | move the attempt machine INTO follow (frozen calls in a durable record beside the Store, not the trie): "no new call while one is open" becomes a theorem; most Rust decision sites vanish; the relay row shrinks to bytes | 1–2 months |

Note the convergence: PROTOCOL's and RUST's changes are the same change seen from two sides (the command/attempt state lives in follow, typed; the client holds a handle). HOST's and HOSTING's are the same (one re-deriver, one journal of what it absorbed). GATES's and LANGPROOFS's are the same (a spec tier with pinned claims). Three structural moves, not nine.

## 8. The one thing to STOP, per layer

Building kernel acts no receiver produces (KERNEL). Landing contextual theory nothing deployed reads (LANG). Treating "a plant reds it" as evidence a statement is right (LANGPROOFS, GATES). Answering each finding with a new scripted row and another harness flag, now 30 (PROTOCOL). Proving theorems about models that sit BESIDE the code (`WriterLife`, `IdentityImage`, the memory checkpoint, host-sim's toy machine, Kernel's audit standing in for the follower) (HOST). Adding partial theorems each with its own "not claimed" list before the end-to-end Bell spec is written (HOSTING). Writing any Rust that handles a byte layout by hand, including inside Lean templates (CODEC). Writing Rust stand-ins of Lean behaviour and calling them plants (RUST). Treating every theorem as trusted: drop map lines for lemmas no claim depends on (GATES).

## 10. What this means for what we do next (ROOT's reading, not the readers')
