(2) What I think Mini is likely to get wrong:
- Three different acknowledgements: a transport ACK, a retention receipt, and an application outcome mean different things (architecture.md §Purpose). fn accepting a POST is not Mini's commit; the consumer owns its own durable inbox/outbox and processing transaction; fn preserves bytes and delivery undertakings.
- `uncertain` is a real outcome: a lost reply after POST may still be accepted. Recovery is idempotent resend of the same exact bytes (same content identity / Message-ID), never retry under a new identity. Design Mini's call path to carry the operation identity through retries.
- Local article numbers are per-node, per-group and never global; portable identity is the content digest / Message-ID; no ordering across nodes and no last-writer-wins on wall-clock — conflicting evidence is kept.
- fn is a log/feed store, not a mutable KV: no in-place update; withdrawal/cancel is a record; storage is released by retention obligations ("what does this node have, why does it keep it"). Mini's checkpoints-as-objects map to appends + a cursor, not overwrites.
- Codec ownership: fn stores and hashes the exact bytes it is given; the canonical encoding of Mini's payload should be Mini's, and fn should treat it as opaque. The contract across Lean and ACL2 cannot be theorem-to-theorem (different logics): it has to be a codec-level statement both sides check — shared byte vectors and a named boundary function on each side.
- Failure semantics: an ambiguous persistence failure in fn is a recovery event — the node stops (faults) rather than keep mutating; torn writes are handled by the log's crash model and recovery. Mini must treat "fn stopped for recovery" as a normal state, not data loss.
- Throughput and latency: POST is barrier-bound (~0.3 fsync per POST batched on NVMe at 8 writers; F3/F4 still OPEN), the owner serializes mutations, and cold reads go through a page cache. Fine for a durable event log; not a low-latency hot KV.
- Re-genesis: until the format word freezes (`fn-store-1` at the cut), Mini must not treat an fn store as the only copy; plan for export/import across format bumps.
- Trust boundary: the digest is proved in ACL2; signatures, TLS and the OS are trusted primitives — Mini's assurance story should cite them as assumptions, not theorems.
