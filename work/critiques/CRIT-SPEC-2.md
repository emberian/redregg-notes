Before calling the top-level spec complete, I require formal versions of all three apex contracts with the deployed-process refinement proved. The intent contract must follow one authenticated intent through retries, signing, settlement, cancellation, persistence, and restart. The transition contract must cover births, exact authorized effects, issuance, delegated spending, applicable contextual laws, and the declared resource budget. The project is closer to a reviewable trusted statement than it was in the first review because it now exposes more component obligations, but completion still requires the three apex refinement theorems over the actual deployed executions.
