## Technical report

**Architecture, as built.** nanodregg is one Lean definition of a shared-state system with a Rust client that decides nothing. The kernel (`Kernel/`) holds the World: a persistent binary Merkle trie of cells and nullifiers whose root is committed into the chain every turn (the head record is the phone's read, proved premise-free at the deployed instance), a bounded in-window journal (pruned every window; being moved into a second instance of the same trie type by KERNEL-12 so the World codec's canonicity is plain equality and the phone can verify a transaction id landed), members and keys (Ed25519 only, a verifier proved against RFC 8032 end to end, with SHA-512 trusted by vectors), one grant kind with owner-gated invoke and public grants that are used but never re-delegated, value with wells, floors, credit and a one-nullifier escrow, and the carry (re-genesis of a World from an exported, digest-checked file, with ids kept opaque by theorem). Every receiver takes an actor, so a method's effects run under the calling object's authority (`effect_as_signed`), and every signed message carries its own domain. The codec layer (`Codec/`, `Emit/`) is one stream-codec family with a canonicity theorem per site, a refine census that refuses a new refinement without a lawfulness theorem, a vector table that detects a change of meaning under an unchanged tag, and generators for the Rust tables (client, launcher, broker). The host (`Host/`) is the resident loop over a framed log, a sealed whole-World base, and a fixed-entry MAC'd index; open verifies the origin frame and one index entry, every missing or corrupt file is a named status, a follower reopens through the same path as the writer, and export/import are the one carry decision shared with the conformance rows. The protocol layer (`Protocol/`) is the operation table, the command language (now including `sync`, the four ledger verbs, and an exit table), the read envelope signed over its own window, and 21 conformance rows that are the specification. The language (`Lang/`) is Objective Bend with a `digest` type, declared call slots per method, an `adopt` occasion, a reader and one printer with a proved round trip for every installable package, and a required-step counter that makes THE BAR's waste ratio a theorem-backed measurement. Hosting (`Hosting/`, `launcher/`) is the grain lifecycle package, a controller that tails the host, a launcher that spawns only through the sandbox crate, and a key broker that holds the operator's provider key behind TLS with operator-data anchors, meters allowances read from the World, and never lets a key byte reach a resident (the canary control). The client (`client/`, `sandbox/`, `attach/`, `devtools/`) is `nd`, its supervisor, custody records (member, hosted, and the new envelope-only observer), one confinement shape per platform with its facts recorded (exec and connect are not filtered on Linux), and a socket allowance of at most two paths.
