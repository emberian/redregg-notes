hbox safety (verbatim house rules): run every build as `swarm-build <cmd>` (THREADS=4 when loadavg > 16). Never an unfiltered `cargo test`/`-p <crate>` suite; filtered only. Never `git stash`. "Stopping processes: PID file or nothing (incident 2026-10-10). A subagent ran `pkill -f "cat" -u ember` to clear its own stuck `cat`. `-f` matches the whole command line, every app lives under `/Appli**cat**ions`, and 44 of my apps and agents got SIGTERM. Never kill by pattern. No `pkill -f <anything>`, no `pkill -x <bare word>`, no `killall`, no `kill -u`. A process you started is stopped by the PID in its PID file, and only that. Every detached job writes one (`nohup ... & echo $! > job.pid`). No PID file, no kill: tell me instead. If you must look first, `pgrep -fl` and read the list; it is never the argument to a kill." Waiting: a wait loop is `while kill -0 $(cat job.pid); do sleep 60; done` on a PID file you wrote, never a self-matching `pgrep -f`, never `until [ … ]; do :; done` (a busy spin; one burned a core for hours on 10-14), never a grep-the-log loop without a bound. Every loop sleeps and has a deadline. Disk: /tank under 50G free → stop and report; the Mac's data volume is nearly full: write nothing large on the Mac; delete nothing you did not create.
