  (field : code|args|caller|cap)`), the whole call tree co-failing (call = atomic co-failure). Reasons: (1) doctrine: a refusal is

  concern is weak: the grant is authored by the signer and the mismatching args come from the caller's own program, so the
  refusal tells the submitter nothing it did not choose; (3) consistent with `.grantSpent` for an exhausted grant.
