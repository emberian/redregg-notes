## 2. The writer model (replaces the charter footnote)

**(a) Many Worlds, one writer each, receipts between Worlds — IN the plan (P5).** Each World keeps law 1. Between Worlds, knowledge moves as RECEIPTS (PROTOCOL-45 `Receipt.build/check`), checked by the Lean runtime against a HEAD CELL of A that B holds (written by a member of B that follows A, under A's sponsor-signed origin, PROTOCOL-61). The receipt rides as a method ARGUMENT (no new `Receive.Body` constructor: call kinds at ceiling 13); the runtime synthesizes `receipts : tx → verified` exactly as E1 synthesizes `cited` (one mechanism, two uses). `foreign_receipt_sound`: B admits with `verified = true` against head H, H is the head of some `audit_ok`-honest fold of A ⇒ tx admitted in A at that height. Premise: head freshness (thin-member trust). A forged receipt refuses `pathMismatch`; a second head of A at the same height with different roots refuses `forkedHead` when posted. Buys asynchronous cross-host protocols; NOT atomic cross-host commitment (a row needing that is E6/E7's trigger). Price on landed theorems: zero.

**(b) Replicated agreement (E7) — a PRICED, PLANNED phase (P6), offered as the path to writer availability.** One decided log per World, so `stale_behind_forever` (`Kernel/Proofs/Versions.lean:240`), `settle_final_never_admits` (`Protocol/Proofs/Settle.lean:201`), `staleBehind_final` (`Protocol/Proofs/Behind.lean:219`), `audit_ok` (`Kernel/Proofs/Audit.lean:275`) keep their statements. The hypothesis "the folded log is a prefix of the decided log" lands on every follower and settlement path and on `WriterLife`/`writer_life_trusted` (`Host/Proofs/Trusted.lean:239, :269`), restated over "the decided log's writer"; law 4's lock text stops being the safety argument; KERNEL-44's recorded refusal is final once the refusal STEP is decided. Spec: mini's Generic Simplex (safety over the executable step). **Liveness is not a theorem anywhere and will not be**: deliverable = safety + a measured availability row + the one-host plant (one host alone must refuse to decide). `host!`+`protocol!` flag day; every Store re-geneses; Rust gains a vote transport (law 2). INFERRED 6–9k Lean + 2–3k Rust, 2–3 lane-weeks.

**(c) Merge of divergent histories (E8) — REFUSED, with the theorems it breaks:** every theorem over `World.fold` extensions loses its object: `stale_behind_forever`, `settle_final_never_admits`, `staleBehind_final`, `object_history_chain`, `audit_ok`'s `Honest`, LEDGER-15's at-most-one payout, `grant_moves_le_cap` (`Ledger/Proofs/GrantCap.lean:256`). `admit_comm` commutes two Turns at one World and gives no merge. **(d) Charter scope item 3, rewritten:** "**Writers.** One writer per World; followers re-derive every Turn (law 1, PROTOCOL-4). Many Worlds are in scope: a protocol between Worlds runs on receipts checked against held heads; it has no atomic cross-World commitment. Replication of a World's writer by agreement is a planned, priced phase that keeps one decided log per World; liveness is measured, never proved. Merging divergent histories of one World is refused: a fork is a new World."
