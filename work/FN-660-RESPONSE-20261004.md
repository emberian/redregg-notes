**Corrections.**

- **No rule conflicts with Mini's shape:**
  - Unknown headers, including `Content-Type` and `Content-Transfer-Encoding: base64`, pass.
  - Header bytes must be SP, HTAB or VCHAR; CRLF is strict; a bare CR or LF is `:invalid-header` (`specs/article-parser.md:33-38`).

  - POST requires From, Subject and Newsgroups. A supplied Path, Xref, Injection-Date or Injection-Info is refused (`protocol-table.lisp:997-1007`).
  - Base64 lines never begin with `.`, and long body lines pass.
- **QF-7.**
  - Injection performs **no Date check**: "fn has no date-time parser" (`books/injection.lisp:26`).
  - With Date and Message-ID both supplied, no Injection-Date is added, so the injected article does not depend on the clock (`:29-31`).
  - Expiry keys on `Expires:` or the acceptance stamp, never on Date (`books/expiry.lisp:13-21`).
  - Peers refuse only Date more than 24 h in the future (`books/peer-inbound.lisp:48,584`).
  - **No path refuses an article as too old.**
  - The version word is fn's choice. We propose a header: it is inside the signed source, and it is checkable without a body parse.

  - Article size A is a live store-profile field: `fn admin policy set max-article-octets N` (STO-038, `specs/storage.md:65-100`).

### §3.3 Identity, and SN-2 (the 32,768 figure)

**Correction: T = 32,768 is not a default.**
- A capacity-free `init` (every mission) takes the largest friend rung that fits: H ∈ {64, 32, 16} MiB with **T = H/512** (`books/heap-reservation.lisp:3-20,40`, PKT-707). The live nodes' 32,768 is the **16 MiB rung**.
- A bare `init` defaults T to 2^32−1 (`docs/operator-internals.md:195`).
- Per D27 (`decisions.md:1194-1201`), T, H and A are all operator store-profile fields. They are raised by `policy set max-transactions|max-history-octets|max-article-octets` (STO-038, `specs/storage.md:67-83`; PRF-940):
  - applied live if the new heap figure fits the process's reservation;
  - otherwise recorded and effective at next start, with no data moved;
  - refused by name if the machine cannot hold it.

**H binds alongside T, and SN-2 counts only T.**
- The admission gate is "committed record octets + prospective ≤ H" (`books/byte-store-frame.lisp:372-378`).
- At the 16 MiB rung, archive lifetime is min(T/blocks-per-article, 16 MiB/bytes-per-block) × b.
- **Bundling K blocks per article saves:**
  - transactions;
  - per-record heap: the heap figure charges 12 KiB per record + 320 per group membership, ×2 (`operator-internals.md:236-240`); measured 3.6 KB per record after reopen;
  - open time, which is about linear in records (F6: 20k in 8 s, 40k in 16–22 s).
- **Bundling does not save H.** The operator raises H as well as T.

**Other corrections to SN-2.**
- "A store admits one transaction fewer" is not established. The gate is `committed-count < T` (`:372`).
- `fnce` bootstrap and register records also take txids. The usable count is T minus the setup records.

**QF-9.**
- Yes, each accepted article is one transaction.
- **Each consumer ack is one transaction plus ≤ 364 octets of H** (`specs/consumer-progress.md:132-133, 532-536`). Register, rebase and unregister cost one each.
- Reclaim and compaction free bytes, **never transactions** (`operator-internals.md:488-489,516-519`).
- Largest T under `MemoryMax=2G`: not computed in tree. It is S4's measurement.

**The bundle size must also respect:**
- A, the article bound (QF-8);
- the poll page budget of 262,144 returned article octets, which is "proposed v1 policy", not measured (`consumer-progress.md:122-126`), if followers read through the cursor;
- the `:oversize` rule: a single event the poll reply cannot carry is refused by name, with no skip contract (PKT-466, `:127-130,700-701`).

### §3.5 Failure semantics, SN-5, QF-3
