## CLIENT-54 · the Vault demo: graded effects and held rows on the page, their outcomes read off the kernel's own menu (2026-10-27, GLM-I)

**Decision.** The testground could not show LANG-89 or LANG-90: no demo drove a graded method or a holds relation, and the inspector showed neither a method's grade nor a relation's held field. The demo is the journey's own Vault: the kernel reads `Lang.Journey.vaultSurface` with the journey's own reader and ships the text `install` takes (`tg_text "vault"`), and it answers the scene's expectations as a menu (`tg_holds`): each checked command's outcome, the refusal's words from the kernel's own checkers (`Lang.checkMoves` on the package's grade, `Lang.checkLegsGo` on its relation label), and the balances and rows the §HV rows assert. The scene (`testground/scenes-vault.js`, loaded by one line in `index.html`) types alice's commands and writes none of its expectations; the gate (`scenes-vault-run.mjs`) is red until the scene follows a changed grade, label or wording. The inspector gains a grade column (`tg_iface`) and a holds line beside each relation, and an account opens a window of its own (`tg_cell` answers owner, asset, balance and floor through `Kernel.Value.ofCell`), so the demo's balances are shown beside its refusals as live windows, not sentences. Rejected: a JS reading of the package's effects section (a second implementation that drifts, the same reason every other answer is an export), and a scene that asserted its own expectations in JS (a row that changes would leave the scene green and lying).

**Critique.** The menu's refusal words come from calling the checkers on a synthetic one-element case: that shares their format strings but not the run's own path, so a checker whose words changed is caught only by the gate's live comparison. The balance and row numbers are transcribed from the journey's notes, not read out of them: a journey that changed §HV1's amounts would leave the menu stale until the refusal words moved and the gate went red. The end-state read covers all three checks only because the scene moves no value after the deposit. And the menu is the Vault's alone; a second graded package would want this generalized, which this does not attempt.

**Status.** landed (this commit). Re-emits `testground/kernel.{mjs,wasm}`; nothing on the wire changed.
