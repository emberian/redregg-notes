testground: the Vault, a demo of graded effects and held rows — the journey's own package, its outcomes read off the kernel

The testground had no demo of LANG-89 or LANG-90: nothing on the page showed a method's grade
bounding what one run may move, or a relation whose rows name value the object holds. Now it has
one. The Vault is the journey's own package (Lang/Journey/Holds.lean, the HV rows): the kernel
reads its surface text with the journey's own reader and ships the text install takes (tg_text
"vault"), and it answers the scene's expectations as a menu (tg_holds): each checked command's
expected outcome, the refusal's exact words produced by the kernel's own checkers (Lang.checkMoves
on the journey's grade, Lang.checkLegsGo on its relation label), and the balances and rows the
journey's rows assert. The scene (testground/scenes-vault.js, loaded by one line in index.html)
types alice's commands and writes none of its expectations: she issues the denomination, creates
the vault, opens the accounts, funds hers with 50 and grants the vault object the right to move up
to 100 from it; a deposit that pays its leg is admitted, with the row read back and the balances
shown in the account windows; a withdraw of 1001 under a grade of 1000 is refused (effectExceeded)
and nothing moves, the balances and the row unchanged beside the refusal; a deposit that inserts a
leg of 10 and pays nothing is refused (legUnfunded) and no row appears. Every result prints inline
beside its command. The gate runs the scene against the kernel's menu
(testground/scenes-vault-run.mjs, gate.sh), so a grade, label or wording that changes in the kernel
or the journey turns the scene red until it follows; a tour step plays the demo.

The inspector gains the two fields it could not show: a method's grade (tg_iface, a moves column in
Methods) and a relation's held field (a line beside each relation), both read off the member's
follow. An account opens a window of its own (tg_cell answers an account's owner, asset, balance
and floor through Kernel.Value.ofCell), which is how the demo's balances stay visible. publish.sh
carries the scene file. Rebuilds the testground kernel (testground/kernel.mjs, kernel.wasm are build
outputs); nothing on the wire changed.
