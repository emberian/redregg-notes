The testground now shows the market (DESIGN-CTX §2.5b): a book of `Std.offerEscrow`
offers, the contextual design's second consumer of `Req.caller` and LANG-82's legs.

create a book, issue a coin and post matching offers (give 5 for 7, price 9 inside

matching pair pays his 7 to alice and her 5 to bob in one Turn, both books' offers
gone, the balances read before and after (LE1). The third member carol joins the
founders at the page's and the harnesses' genesis, so her take is signed in the same
World as the books.
