# The axes past the frontier (ROOT, real 2026-10-26, under Ember's goal: "what question could I ask myself if I was smarter that would drive this past the frontier on every (metaprogramming etc) axis")

Two Fables hold two axes already: FABLE-CTX (the contextual theory at full power) and FABLE-TRUST (the trust model past one box). The axes below are the ones I had been treating as fixed. Each gets a Fable with goal + material and no method; each designs and builds.

1. METAPROGRAMMING. nano's packages are written in a Lisp-shaped surface over a CEK machine with hand-counted de Bruijn generators (StdHole, StdActivity, StdOffer are Lean functions that EMIT terms). The LANG reader called the generators "hand-counted de Bruijn indices" and an embarrassment. The frontier question: what if packages could generate packages, laws could be derived from specifications, and the generators were themselves nano programs judged by the kernel (staged computation; MetaML/Template Haskell lineage; Racket's macro tower; Lean's own elaborator as the model), so that "install a generator" is a Turn and a generated package carries a receipt of its generation? What does a self-describing, self-extending World look like, where the Std library is itself installed objects rather than Lean-emitted constants?

2. THE PROOF SYSTEM ITSELF. 6,000 theorems, hand-proved by agents, with a judge. The frontier question: what would it take for the KERNEL to check proofs about programs INSTALLED in it, so that a member can ship a package WITH a proof (that its law preserves an invariant; that its method conserves value) and the World refuses an unproved claim rather than trusting a docstring (proof-carrying code, Necula 1997; Nexus's authorization logic; F*/Dafny-style verified contracts; Lean itself as the checker via a reflected kernel)? Is a reflected checker of a fragment of Lean's logic inside nano's term language feasible, and what fragment buys the most?

3. THE MEMBER'S EXPERIENCE AND AGENTS AS MEMBERS. mini's ambition was "people and agents". nano's member is a key, a follower and an nd CLI. The frontier question: what is the smallest thing that makes an LLM agent a first-class member (a resident with a budget, holes it fills, laws that constrain what it may do, receipts for everything it did) so that nano is the substrate the agent lives in rather than a tool it calls? The Bell is a hint: the resident answers rings. What is the generalisation where the resident is an agent and the ring is any obligation?

4. SELF-MEASUREMENT AND THE OBSERVATORY. The gates, census, waste pins and bar are outside the system (Python over the tree). The frontier question: can the World measure itself: the waste counters as kernel-maintained cells, the size ratchet as a law, the convergence verdict as a receipt a member can check, so that "is this system healthy" is a query a member makes of the World, not a script ROOT runs on a box?

5. TIME AND THE OUTSIDE WORLD. nano's clock is the height; notBefore/validUntil are heights; the only outside input is a member's signature. The frontier question: oracles and the real clock as judged inputs: a signed external fact (a timestamp, a price, a document hash) as a cell with a provenance the law can require, so that an escrow can settle on an outside event; what is the trust model for an oracle member, and does E5's receipts-between-Worlds already give it (an oracle is a World)?

6. COMPOSITION OF WORLDS. E5 gives receipts between Worlds; the trust design is deciding the model. The frontier question beyond it: a World that is a MEMBER of another World (a resident that is itself a World; a federation where the root World's law judges the member Worlds' heads), so that "scaling toward mini" is recursive rather than flat.

7. THE LANGUAGE'S OWN LIMIT. Terms, no types beyond the data codec's; holes typed; laws judge old→new. The frontier question: dependent types in the surface (a law that is a type; a method whose type states its effect; the kernel's Call.Fits as a type a package carries), effect systems (what a method may touch, declared and checked, the Move footprint the reader named), and linear value in the TYPE rather than in the kernel's count (the thing PROT-8 declined for lack of a reader: is the reader here?).
