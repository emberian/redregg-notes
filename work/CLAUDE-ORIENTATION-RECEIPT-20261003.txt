BEND: CLAUDE'S ASSESSMENT (INFERRED; for Ember's decision, not decided)
The question "elaborate into the kernel vs inject Bend into Objective (a
self-interpreter)" presupposes BendTT is the kernel. For the live system it
cannot be: BendTT's theorems are about ITS strict total Eval. A lazy fix, a
cyclic self, a shared non-Data closure have no image in it except through a
fuelled interpreter Book — and then the kernel certifies only that the
interpreter halts per fuel step, which runBounded already has in Lean, and
says nothing about Objective's meaning. BendTT also isn't a proof language
you'd want to do that metatheory in (Type:Type). Gen-1 shows the only direct
elaboration is the total/acyclic/Data-capture fragment, i.e. not the language.
Core4 is already the kernel in every sense that matters: ~1.66k lines of
trusted definitions, a certifying checker (a checker bug can only reject),
adequacy + preservation proven, completeness in progress. The backends
(native lazy machine, oblivious, zk trace, circuits, FHE) consume stepRaw, and
the zk lane has already re-targeted at it. So: NO interpreter in either
direction. The metatheory that is "really-really worth it" is the one in
flight plus three named gaps: (1) finish completeness (leaf76 onward);
(2) grow Core4's data — sums/lists/label case — with preservation/adequacy
cases for each new constructor (linear work in the existing proof files),
rather than keeping BendTT alive as the data calculus; (3) make the
TypeScript elaborator's trust boundary explicit: either port it to Lean with
an adequacy theorem, or keep the Doc r24 pinned-frontend-replay as THE named
trusted frontend (import and invoke included), which the root already flagged.
What BendTT still earns: a reference calculus and a template for the proof
architecture (Par/Pars/confluence/certifying check), and its generic
upper-stack machinery (circuit/Emit/SSA, oblivious memory, PCS/quotient,
activity custody) which must be re-targeted at stepRaw regardless. It does
NOT earn a public cost bound: the records already say termination is not a
resource bound. If a total fragment with circuit-friendly bounds is wanted,
a total fragment OF CORE4 (a structural-descent or fuel criterion checked by
the same certifying checker) is cheaper than a second kernel.
