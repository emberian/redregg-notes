### T3: THE demo app, "Commission Board" (first-class track)

**What it is.** A guild posts paid commissions, members claim them, an agent does the work, and a reviewer
approves. It is built from parts that already run natively, so T3 is composition rather than new kernel
(READ: world/bounty/README.md, docs/OBJECTIVE-BEND.md sections Activities, Calls and sends, Seats).
- **Packages (multi-module, package laws):**
  - `world/commission/Board.obend`: lists commissions. Its law is that a commission's reward is `writeOnce`.
  - `Escrow.obend`: `BountyEscrow` as the seat contract. The reward is HELD by the seat kernel; the escrow wants
    a WORK RECEIPT asset or a refund.
  - `Review.obend`: a reviewer object that answers through `call`, built from `world/workshop/CatalogReview`.
  - `Guild.obend`: a membership domain whose required exits are explicit at registration (GPT-6 5.2).
- **Scoped grants:**
  - The poster grants a fleet agent a `capped` grant to claim and submit on one board, and nothing else.
  - The signer display shows what the cap leaves unconstrained.
- **Async sends:** submit `send`s a notice with postage to the reviewer's inbox. The answer slot carries the
  verdict back.
- **Retries:** the claim is submitted through `mini fleet-sign`. An exact retry after a lost reply returns the
  original receipt (T1.5).
- **Pay:** a JPAY4 fixture-rail deposit funds the poster's Book account.
- **Upgrade:** Board v1 is upgraded to v2 (a new field, `tags`) while a commission is OPEN. The drained judgment
  keeps its obligations.
- **SPK tie-in (optional, D3):** the sntfy grain from `grain-journey.sh` gets a notice on each state change, so the
  demo is visible in a browser through `spk-browser-proxy`.

**Journeys (R17).** One driver, `native/resource-client/commission-demo-journey.py`, on a fresh Store, all
through the shell host. Its rows:
- **CB1 happy path:** post, claim, submit, review approves, the claimant is paid once, and the Book total is
  unchanged.
- **CB2 crash and restart:** the Host is SIGKILLed between submit and review; it reopens and the same obligations
  are pending.
- **CB3 charged failure:** a malformed review plan is charged its public price and nothing else.
- **CB4 lost reply:** an exact retry and a fresh-signature retry both return the same disposition, and nothing is
  re-charged.
- **CB5 cancellation:** cancel before a claim refunds the poster; cancel after a claim is refused.
- **CB6 upgrade:** v1 to v2 mid-commission; the open escrow still pays and refunds as before.
- **CB7 silence:** the deadline exit pays the claimant with no reviewer.
- **CBP plants:** a contract that pays twice, and an upgrade that drops the escrow obligation. Each must be
  refused by name.
- GPT-6 T3's "fan-out/depth bounds over the app's actual message histories" is a theorem over CB1-CB7's
