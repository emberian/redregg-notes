EXPLICIT HUMAN DESTINATION: OBJECTIVE BEND
October3 Ember: "Objective Bend" is clearly where we are headed as a destination.
Root interpretation to develop with Ember: a coherent authored language and live
world combining Bend with first-class partial specifications/prototypes, modules,
composition/finalself/super, source-defined interfaces/protocols, reflection,
governed persistence/effects and evolution. The name does not preselect syntax.
It is more than an optional evaluator behind externally prepared OO metadata.

World-language and OO owners jointly own the language design, with composition,
compiler/proof, docuverse and actual workshop consumers. Converge representative
member-authored programs and a clear boundary: constructs elaborated into exact
Bend core, Mini host/authority/lifetime contracts, and any genuine semantic
extensions needing new mechanized proofs. Reusable specification Data and affine
per-invocation closures are a construction hypothesis; do not declare complete
expressiveness merely because that representation works for small examples.
Persistent reactive lifetime is not silently one total infinite Bend invocation.
If explicit source state machines cannot faithfully provide desired lazy/reactive
semantics, identify the needed extension and prove it. Preserve current receiving
and source checkpoints while developing the fuller language. All relevant owners
were explicitly informed; this direction survives compaction.
