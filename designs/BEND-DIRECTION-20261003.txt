Objective Bend has its own language design. Bend supplies a starting calculus,
not an immutable ceiling on types, quantities, recursion or representation.
Design the intended modularity, effects and persistent computation first; use
faithful embeddings where appropriate and change the core where required.
Data-only reusable captures, acyclic linking and total within-turn evaluation
are properties of the current embedding, not requirements imposed by Ember.
A changed calculus needs explicit semantics, typing and new metatheory. Do not
inherit Bend's consistency or termination results for constructs they do not
cover. General recursive computation must not silently become evidence for
arbitrary logical propositions. Preserve historical executable meaning through
versioned profiles; that does not freeze the new language.

The Fable council is an active design conversation, not an approval oracle.
Root owns the choice and the correspondence between intended semantics,
implementation, proofs and actual member-authored consumers.
