The desired computing world
Ember is building a shared multiuser agentic worldhost whose inhabitants can author,
inspect, extend and govern their tools and places. A source editor, research desk,
community institution, resident and hosted application are demanding users of the
same underlying medium. Independent people and communities retain different rights,
private state and histories. The aim is not exhausted by a successful workshop
example. Mini is the construction home; Bread supplies both valuable precedents
and failures to understand. Nock remains supported; Objective Bend becomes central.

The Houyhnhnm direction is a demand for coherence across the computing experience:
computation, representation, persistence, reflection and extension should be things
the environment can express and reason about. In this project's interpretation,
that means source-authored tools and views, inspectable/recomposable behavior and
continuing computation. It does not mean that a collection of OO feature names or
a self-describing status record already realizes Faré's whole programme. Exact
primary-source interpretation belongs in the Faré argument, not this shorthand.

Why Faré changes the language design
Open extensions have meaning before a complete object exists. Context/final self,
inherited target and resulting target can vary independently. Composing extensions
then closing their common self permits future overrides to affect inherited
behavior; whole super and repeated composition have real operational consequences.
First-class incomplete specifications must remain inspectable and extensible even
when their target is incomplete or divergent. These requirements exceed the former
acyclic link-time tables and numeric method carriers.

An equation x=f(x) does not specify a useful operational construction. Lazy demand,
shared thunk identity, cyclic self and independently delayed fields require actual
semantics. The new call-by-need graph machine is one chosen implementation; proving
it realizes an independent source meaning is required. The retained BendTT CBV
machine is a distinct edition with distinct proofs, not an automatic backend for
the new calculus. Ember explicitly permits changing Bend's types and core.

Resource ownership and laws constrain this freedom without replacing it. A reusable
closure cannot conceal a duplicated affine obligation. General recursion must not
silently inhabit total logical proofs. An extension may intentionally change an
old law; requiring preservation is a separately selected compatibility contract
whose provider needs evidence. Code evolution, object identity, an in-flight
continuation and authority to adopt a revision are separate relationships.

Why privacy is several constructions
Keeping payload bytes secret does not hide who talks to whom, when they act, which
objects conflict, whether an operation was refused, or which repair traffic follows.
A packet observer, malicious operator, computation party and authorized probing
caller have different observations and powers. Encryption, scheduled cover/mixes,
private execution, authenticated audience changes and private recovery each address
different parts of that problem. None supplies the others merely by sharing a key.

Cover must include reads, control, replies and recovery. A schedule that emits
perfect dummy traffic but cannot sustain useful service is an implementation
failure, not successful privacy at an irrelevant cost. Capacity classes, cohorts,
lifetimes, allowed failure leakage and corruption assumptions belong in the claim.
PQ transport ingredients do not establish PQ security for classical-ROM MPC/zk
constructions. Private computation backends must realize the chosen language's
exact meaning, numeric/resource behavior and disclosure contract.

Recovery is part of confidentiality: retries must not reuse consumed correlations,
mix share generations or redispatch uncertain effects. Revocation cannot erase
knowledge. Rewrapping an already-used key discloses irreversibly, so authorization
must be committed before ciphertext leaves custody; a later failed CAS is too late.

Why agreement is scoped to actual dependencies
Independent objects and communities should not need one global governor or one
worldwide order merely to coexist. Conversely, a method spanning several domains
must include every real dependency: reads, absence predicates, aliases, overridden
behavior, current law, fees and resource promises. A proof over an incomplete
manifest is not permission to omit a necessary participant.

Local agreement establishes a domain's durable decision. A joint protocol composes
the necessary domains' actual promises, followed by installation and independently
authorized release. Agreement on bytes, an installed effect, recoverable private
custody and delivery to a caller are different facts. Ordinary asynchronous messages
use durable outboxes and later recipient admission; they are not transactions that
hold locks across arbitrary external IO.

The current Simplex baseline is a concrete construction choice, not a literature
verdict that it dominates all alternatives. Bread blocklace/joint-turn/interfederation
remain substantive comparators. Preserve the research into fairness, synchrony,
thresholds, committee changes and lower bounds, including reasons an alternative
was deferred. Network liveness alone does not establish fair application inclusion.
Changing epochs cannot erase old possible certificates, reservations or owed outputs.

What makes the pieces one system
One authored invocation must retain its program/source edition, exact authority,
observations, resource contract, effects and result through execution, agreement,
installation, private custody and release. A backend certificate is useful only
when its statement is the statement the actual receiver needs. A common proof
carrier, digest or opcode name is not that connection. This is why source-derived
compilation, mechanized refinement and inhabited end-to-end scenarios are all
necessary and why component PASS must not become a substitute destination.

The experiences that test this argument are deliberately different: an extensible
research/workdesk instrument; a shared contested object or authored institution;
and a continuing home/service relationship with recoverable custody. None should
require writing another operator-only runtime for each new behavior. Their fuller
arguments and falsifiers live in designs/ALTERNATIVES-20261003.txt and
designs/SHARED-CONTRACTS-20261003.txt.

How to recover understanding instead of reciting a plan
For the next substantive choice, be able to explain (1) the human capability it
serves, (2) the source semantics and observer/fault model, (3) why the chosen
construction addresses those, (4) the best rejected or deferred alternative and
its tradeoff, (5) which claim is unproved or currently refuted, and (6) an experiment
or theorem that would change the choice. Follow original paper/source passages for
the disputed premise; an agent synopsis is navigation, not primary confirmation.
