HYPOTHESES BEFORE PREFERENCE

H1. A community-authored station is the strongest way to discover whether shared
programmability can support an inhabited place rather than a collection of demos.
H2. A living workdesk is the strongest way to discover whether the programmable
medium changes how people inquire, make tools and collaborate rather than merely
replacing their chat or document editor.
H3. A workshop commons of portable homes is the strongest way to discover whether
contributed hosting/private resource exchange creates independence rather than
another host-dependent subscription service.

These are genuinely different constructions: their durable centers are an
institutional place, a member's working image, and a federation of service/home
contracts. Their normal journeys, publishing decisions and failure tolerances
differ. They can interoperate through Mini. They are not three generic dashboards
or proposed incompatible kernels. Choosing a first consumer does not choose away
the others: cv 01a0b0ed#761 explicitly declines choosing research/social/compute and
asks for a programmable nexus growing outward, including people's own nodes.

Recovered intent constrains all three. July25 cv 5f419eb8#455 calls SS13 station the
ambition, dungeon an away mission, and Second Life/Thirdroom inspirations. July11
cv 6d1ce594#41 describes authors actually interested in running universes and
players coordinating around Discord. June19 cv 383d2fad#482/#521 describes a
durable, rewindable, literate hypermedia system whose tools themselves are world
objects. June24 cv 01c55763#4 rejects the cozy interface and asks for technical
NT/Pharo-like menus, windows, icons, detail, actuation and spatial persistence.

A. COMMUNITY-AUTHORED STATION

The product promise: people return to a place they can understand, change and run
together. Its creative premise is not that everything is a dungeon, nor that real
work must be disguised as fiction. A station provides shared social continuity
between authored universes, missions and ordinary community activity. Authors may
also publish independent universes; the station is not their compulsory lobby.
Specific bars/crafting/guilds are possible content, not recovered requirements.
Leisure needs room of its own. July13 cv d311217b#65 is a tool-returned human
selection of services-only/no-P2E, with assistant-authored question wording; it
qualifies the temptation to make all play or participation earn tokens. A leisure
room is not a failed workroom. Shared real resources and fictional inventory have
distinct economic meaning even when both use native identity and methods.

A meaningful visit:
Mira arrives in a station, recognizes last week's room layout and her unfinished
proposal, reads a notice authored by someone she knows, and sees the source of a
door's behavior. She has permission to repair the door, but not to manage the
station roster. Sol and Ivo both try to borrow one shared instrument; the world
admits one exact reservation. Ivo's refused action leaves the instrument and his
budget unchanged. They propose a new workshop room, rehearse its behavior, and
invite a bounded narrator to suggest a scene. Members adopt an edit under their
institution's rule. The prose suggestion remains attributed to its author; the
adopted consequence has a separate native receipt. A later mission returns an
allowed projection into the same character identity. The station does not blindly
accept a claim that the mission awarded a valuable asset.

Architecture:
The station is a governed collection of places, entities, membership/role rules,
source packages and institutional records. Each object's governing domain stays
explicit. A place is not an authority root over everything visible in it. A room
contains references to separately governed objects and authorized presentations.
Character identity, participant principal, room membership, fictional role and
economic account are separate coordinates linked through authorized relations.
A GM can narrate without acquiring all players' edit rights. An author can publish
a source package without acquiring the station governor's admission rights.

Shared entities are the decisive extension beyond Mini's existing story. A sealed
story currently gives each player their own progress/inventory under a generated
law; that is substantial useful work, not the proposed shared contested entity
model. A station's tool/object retains its own identity and accepted consequences
across visits. A mission instance pins rules/source at birth and cannot silently
alter the persistent institution. Graduation back to station state consumes a
typed result, current admission rule, exact instance identity and single-use
result identity. Narration, declared victory and resource issuance do not become
equivalent merely because all are native documents.

Publishing:
Source changes have draft, validated proposal, institutional decision, and installed
version. They can have different authors/authorities. Existing instances remain
pinned; an institution explicitly adopts a revision for new actions/instances.
The meaning of a ballot is an authorized collective decision. A factual claim
about the proposed code or mission needs its own evidence. The ballot can approve
a false proposition; ResearchRegime proves that distinction in an inhabited
counterexample. Policy may permit a risky experiment, but its badge must say what
was decided, not silently upgrade the evidence.

Effects/history/custody:
Ordinary place/object methods use the native deterministic evaluator and exact
effects. Narrator/provider work is an asynchronous serviced activity that proposes
content, followed by separately admitted changes. Historical visits and rehearsal
are retained source/view coordinates, not authority to replay live payments or
reinstall old capabilities. Offline drafts can survive loss of write authority;
their eventual publication is judged now. Station archive custody, narration
artifact custody, and secret private successor custody are different obligations.

Resource model:
Charge actual admitted work to explicit participant/institution budgets, with
distinct recovery allowance. Shared scarce items need disjoint reservations or
joint decision, not per-player permission to consume the whole capacity. Private
choices can reveal the authorized outcome without revealing every preference;
traffic/status leaks need an explicit profile. Private simultaneous strategy is
not obtained from encrypted inventories alone. World simulation has public cost
classes and bounded update work; no continuous all-world sweep is assumed.

Receiving discriminator:
Three differently authorized members, one newly authored derived behavior, one
shared contested object, one voted source change, one narrator suggestion, restart
and return. Vary member count rather than baking in demo names. Someone manages a
room while lacking founder replacement rights. A revoked author keeps attributable
history but cannot install the old draft. A mission result cannot be reused.

Falsifiers:
It fails as a station if participants only traverse separate player copies and
their supposedly common consequences never meet. It fails as an authored medium
if authors need operators to write Rust for every new behavior. It fails its own
authority model if GM prose can mint canon/economic effects without admission.
It fails socially if the shared place adds friction and nobody returns except to
complete scripted tests. Recovered authors/player interest is historical interest,
not evidence of current adoption or a supplied alpha launch roster.

Transfer decision:
Reuse authored assets, small syntax/validated-edit data, parent/content attribution,
and useful UI interactions. Semantically migrate collective choice, realm/instance
identity and graduation to Mini's source/law/current-authority path. Preserve
realm-model's actual durable/node admission work; it is not already kernel-enforced
because ruleset_root is absent from Bread Turn and its sample cells are open.
Do not import an EmbeddedExecutor per game or claim a narration signature establishes
truth. Current Mini story is the starting consumer, not something to rebuild.

B. LIVING WORKDESK AND RESEARCH IMAGE

The product promise: your working environment is durable, inspectable and editable
enough that you and collaborators can change the tools of thought while using them.
The center is a member's working image: spatial arrangement, documents, instruments,
branches, running activities and narrowed published facets. Shared workrooms are
relationships between images and independently governed resources, not ownership
of everyone else's image. A social station can be one open workspace, not the
desktop's universal organizing metaphor.

A meaningful session:
Nadia opens yesterday's source-linked notebook. The citations point to exact
fragments; a live section has advanced, and its pinned comparison is still useful.
She opens a definition inspector beside the notebook, changes a small review-card
prototype, and tries it on a local draft. Her colleague sees the public argument
and a locked private fragment. Nadia asks Hermes to compare two revisions under a
bounded mandate. The provider result survives a lost reply. Hermes proposes a
source patch; a real reviewer discusses the exact fragment and approves a revision.
The source changed meanwhile, so installation refuses at the current base, keeping
the draft and original work result. They resolve the change and admit a new exact
installation. On restart, windows/source coordinates and retained work reconnect;
it never implies that arbitrary process RAM or every input event was preserved.

Architecture:
Workspace layout and instrument definitions are separately governed native state.
Transient cursor motion/IME composition may remain local; semantic checkpoints
retain meaningful working arrangements/drafts. The image records references and
activity identities, not a duplicate authoritative copy of each mounted object's
state. Authored renderers consume authorized projections and intent templates.
A view definition is not a read grant, and a clickable action is not an execution
grant. Opening a notebook authorizes neither its transcluded sources nor the tools
it happens to name. Multiple windows/views follow the same identities without
forcing all object types into one generic property-grid interface.

Instruments are ordinary authenticated source objects: definition, schema/codec,
behavior composition, restriction conjunction, supported evaluator and view
packages are explicit. Instantiation pins a source identity. The final-self/super
behavior of a derived review card remains the intended full OO contract; current
ROM numeric-output tables do not supply it. Changes to a view can be low impact;
changes to executable meaning have a validation/publication/adoption boundary.
Unresolved collaborative source must remain a draft, not choose a text projection
and silently seal executable code.

Research discipline:
There are independent axes: source provenance, admitted authority/effects,
reproducible/checkable content, and community approval. A model finding is an
attributed proposal; a reviewer approval is an institution's choice; a checked
certificate has a stated theorem/assumptions; an optimistic conclusion has an
observation window and liveness/completeness obligations. Combining them into one
green badge loses information. This is a concrete application of ResearchRegime,
not a new proof that research can be made universally objective.

Effects/history/custody:
The deterministic object action, external provider request, reviewed source patch,
and physical app export retain linked but distinct operation identities. A work
record ties mandate/source/result; its delivery can happen later. Rehearsal forks
contain suggestions and previews. Settlement means a fresh admitted live change
with the actual installed result; Bread shared_fork's settled_root alone does not
establish that. The UI should support inspecting current law, exact old source,
retained receipts and uncertainty without turning every interaction into a receipt
browser. Source authority is independently checked for each historical or live
fragment. Historical UI persistence and historical read permission are different.

Resource model:
Interactive observation/search has bounded incremental indexes keyed by source,
viewer/authority and dependent coordinates. Renderer caches never become cached
authorization decisions. Provider activities have a quote/budget, started marker,
result custody, exact settlement and separate recovery reserve. A shared workroom
budget cannot become independent copies of the whole allowance. Turning every
mount/open/keystroke into a global joint transaction would sabotage the medium;
joint coordination follows actual effect/dependency domains.

Receiving discriminator:
Create a useful reusable derived instrument inside Mini; render it both as a
notebook section and a technical inspector; run it against differently authorized
source fragments; request resident work; crash after receiving output; review an
exact source proposal; reject stale/currently unauthorized installation; resolve
and install; restart into the same work with exactly one provider charge/result.

Falsifiers:
It fails as a live image if only operator-written CLI verbs extend the world. It
fails as a docuverse if transclusion is copied text that forgets identity or leaks
source authority. It fails collaboration if role downgrades erase previously
admitted authorship or refused edits discard local drafts. It fails portability
if a snapshot is just arbitrary credentials plus host-local process paths.
It fails ergonomically if inspecting and composing real machinery requires more
manual paperwork than the existing shell/editor. Bread's richer technical UI is
evidence of design material, not evidence that its unsafe save or full-cell reads
meet Mini's current authority model.

Transfer decision:
Reuse pure bounded presentation trees, selected text/web lowering, source/patch
carry syntax and mature inspect/act/inspect interactions. Migrate review and branch
semantics to native authority, full principal identities and exact installation.
Use existing Mini source/history/protected-view and resident artifacts as receiving
homes. Abandon out-of-band heap save, universal ledger census and parallel async
notebook runtimes; retain the consumers they were meant to serve. This alternative
can carry meaningful creative activity without making the desk itself a game.

C. WORKSHOP COMMONS OF PORTABLE HOMES

The product promise: people and their bounded agents can keep a home for work,
contribute useful service capacity, negotiate private exchanges, and replace a
provider without losing their acknowledged history or surrendering every role to
the operator. Its center is a federation of independently governed home/service
contracts. It is not a global market for arbitrary ML/numeric workloads: the
October corpus places numerical jobs with Hellas, while Dregg's native authored
computation, Hermes and resource hosting remain the relevant activities.

A meaningful week:
Rao runs a community node and offers a bounded resident home plus storage for
shared research. El rents a home under explicit scope, budget and recovery terms,
and grants a private collaborator one facet. The group collectively funds useful
service without publishing each member's willingness to pay. The service may
operate in a station or workdesk; its contractual meaning is independent of the UI.
El countersigns an exact history checkpoint. The provider later disconnects. The
system distinguishes 'finished and retained but not delivered' from 'uncertain
dispatch' and from 'not admitted.' Successor custody, if qualified, lets El resume
the same work elsewhere, with fresh signing authority and explicit old-provider
fencing. If custody is inadequate, it retains a recoverable obligation/refusal
rather than charging for a fresh fabricated continuation. The commons can elect a
new moderator without gaining the right to decrypt El's private home.

Architecture:
Home identity, continuation history, source package, lease, custody cohort,
execution provider, billing account and community membership are independently
identified. A host chooses what capacity it offers; a renter chooses what work to
authorize; resource law decides which effects are admitted. A moderator acts over
community resources, not by becoming an implicit recovery superuser. Scope can
expand through legitimate grant/factory/self-origin procedures; confinement means
those procedures must carry authority, not that rights can never grow anywhere.
Bread Spec/Authority explicitly models Amplify, Mint, Endow and consented Origin.

Service interfaces distinguish replayable deterministic computation from serviced
external effects. The native evaluator returns exact bounded effects/results. A
provider call admits an obligation, obtains custody/receipts, then materializes a
result under source/current audience law. Discovery/quotes are authorized views;
they are not a promise of actual available capacity. Bounded session transport
leases can make apps useful, but each semantic effect/payment keeps its own exact
admission. An uncertain POST is not replayable merely because its app lease lives.

Markets/governance:
Private allocation combines authorized confidential inputs, a declared feasibility
relation, selected allocation, disjoint capacity reservations and exact settlement.
A solver can propose; a small checker qualifies the result against committed
constraints. Conservation is not fairness, solver optimality or availability.
Ballots control institutional choices under an explicit electorate/policy. They
cannot certify private predicate validity or external payment occurrence. Asset
value entry is a separate receiving relation with its external rail; no synthetic
Book credit or address invented by an agent establishes Solana mainnet receipt.

History/custody:
An acknowledged prefix prevents a provider presenting a shortened or changed
acknowledged history; it does not prove execution completeness, provider honesty,
or storage availability. Current Bread RenterCheckpoint signs head_root+num_turns,
with a domain separator; genesis nonce/key binding is separately platform-recorded
and verifier-pinned. A new Mini acknowledgment should explicitly bind native
resource/genesis/generation/source coordinates, not imply the existing renter
signature already binds those. Mobility exports sealed lawful custody and exact
retained obligations, never SessionCarrier's raw signing seed. Device/source read
audience and MPC successor-holder cohort are separate access structures.

Resource model:
Reservations are disjoint or jointly decided before scarce physical consumption.
Per-participant optimism must not oversell common capacity: SharedResourceBudget
at f=0 gives each honest participant the whole balance, so two can both consume
it before later reconciliation. Economic reserve, physical capacity, traffic
schedule, correlation/pad lifetime and repair service are different resources.
Admission cannot spend the final repair budget needed to discharge an admitted
obligation. Epoch close/GC must carry nullifiers and possible old obligations;
finite retention is a contract with legal retirement, not deletion on a timer.

Receiving discriminator:
Two independent provider domains, three renters, concurrent allocations, an
acknowledged resident history, crash after effect/before reply, provider departure,
current audience change and same-operation successor recovery. Use an allowed
alpha fault/capacity profile, then test the actual receiving and privacy traces.
If qualified provider migration is not implemented, retain an explicit immobile
home instead of a portable label. That is unfinished product construction, not
a reason to misname an archive export as migration.

Falsifiers:
It fails independence if provider replacement needs a secret host credential or
operator intervention that the renter cannot authorize. It fails the commons if
allocation settles money without reserving service or preserving output custody.
It fails privacy if status/discovery/recovery reveals hidden counterparties beyond
the declared leakage. It fails economics if two honest requests both spend one
capacity. It fails institutions if democratic control is presented as technical
truth or moderation can silently rewrite renter history. None is fixed merely by
adding BFT-shaped local flags or compiling a private execution model.

Transfer decision:
Reuse lifecycle vocabulary, acknowledgment/test cases, archive-first exact recovery,
and constrained solver/checker patterns. Migrate custody/export, shared reservations,
private allocation and billing to Mini native contracts. Do not import the whole
House, its object enum or its repeated per-lane HTTP prepare/install/submit stores.
House's stronger factory birth/Expedition SetField paths deserve individual credit;
ordinary EmitEvent anchoring is a narrower state-hash receipt, not app semantics.

CONDITIONAL PREFERENCE, NOT A PRODUCT ELIMINATION

For current Mini construction, B has the shortest path to a substantial composition
because source-authorized documents, apps, exact resident work and technical
observations already have receiving homes. Its strongest value is bringing tools
inside the medium, not polishing an inspector. A is the necessary contrasting
consumer: it reveals shared contested state, creative authorship and collective
consequence that B could avoid. The combination should be two experiences drawing
on one current semantics, not a combined station-workdesk brand or two fixtures
declared the destination. C should influence today's custody/reservation/resource
interfaces even when its public exchange/migration experience takes longer.

If source-authoring composition cannot actually support A/B, the bottleneck is
native programmable semantics. If B works but A's shared consequences do not,
world/instance law and result admission need work. If both work but provider
replacement needs raw host secrets or doubles billing, C exposes the next system
boundary. These are discriminators with consequences, not a popularity ranking.

The October13 recovered profile remains a variable-member platform, complete
full-OO/private execution/traffic privacy/agreement prototypes, substantial
docuverse/deos, actual usable apps/Hermes and operation/recovery. Traffic privacy
and some agreement are explicit alpha conditions. All alternatives consume that
work rather than moving it into later documentation. Their richer station,
publication, service migration and commons experiences are design hypotheses.
October26 and November sequences in earlier reports are proposals, not recovered
feature deadlines. Neither portable full desktop/seL4 parity nor a universal
proof stack is silently added as an October gate.

Actual current feedback from authors/participants/operators; complete Mini native
