creative tools and resident services ordinary authored Bend objects on the same
kernel. Starbridge is their common authoring/inspection/actuation environment;
specialized market, story, notebook and home views remain distinct experiences.

trusted market universe. Their useful mechanisms should become shared source
modules and qualified execution adapters. This is a broad outward-growing nexus,
not a choice of only social software, research notebooks or compute exchange.

(B) an authored world/tool studio with collective review, and (C) a resident and
service commons. Each exercises private inputs, source-defined behavior, actual
effects, retained evidence and human-readable recovery. They share infrastructure
because they share semantics, not because every activity must become a market.
Keep future dregg-old/dregg renaming out of this construction cycle.

The three constructions
=======================

A. A community allocation and exchange room
------------------------------------------
A group needs to allocate expedition equipment, studio time, contributed hosting
capacity, or scarce places in a joint activity. A member creates an AllocationRoom
from an inspectable Bend module, chooses a mechanism already authorized by the
community, identifies the actual resources and capacity sources, and publishes
the round. Members submit private limits/preferences or choose public orders.
Their wallet holds the private input and receipt; the host shows the contracted
round status. At close, a solver proposes an allocation, the selected execution
plan checks the exact rule, and the kernel reserves/installs actual transfers or
resource entitlements. Each person receives their own result; the shared room
publishes only its declared aggregate/result. A game can immediately use the
awarded item; a home can use the allocated service reservation.

This is not restricted to a trading terminal. Community choice can select the
policy or spend envelope; markets can allocate within it; authored games can use
the outcome. Voting supplies institutional consent, not evidence that an
allocation is feasible, fair, available or optimal. A rich domain may use no
price at all. The room must retain those separate authorities visibly.

First mechanism decision: preserve the existing Mini single-seller price/slot
auction as one named mechanism, but move its complete allocation rule into
source-authoritative execution. Add DrEX two-sided uniform-price/pro-rata as a
separate module. They are different economics: silently replacing one with the
other changes winners and incentives. Bind supply, grid, eligibility, order
ordering, tie policy, zero/overflow policy, close/release windows and payment
rule in the module/source instance. Public policy choices are not solver knobs.

Private execution is not an optional badge on the same plaintext transaction:
* The order's exact ciphertext/commitment, owner entitlement, resource reservation,
  batch inclusion/order and semantic input must be bound. A correct calculation
  on a substituted book is the wrong action. Private range/balance admission
  and duplicate/withdraw rules are obligations of intake, not the optimizer.
* A private result needs its own retained return/release slots. The public status
  may reveal price/volume, existence, capacity or failure only as specified. A
  winner may be private in one room and deliberately public in another.
* Allocating resources in separate governed domains uses the current joint
  reservation/install protocol, not a market-owned clone-and-swap. No public
  receipt lets a market bypass the target's rule or current audience policy.

B. An authored world and tool studio
----------------------------------
A member opens a community notebook, room, game prop or market policy, inspects
its source, forks a template with attribution, edits a Bend method and makes an
instance. The same object has a domain view for use, a source view for learning
and authoring, and an activity/history view for understanding effects. Another
member proposes a revision, delegates review to a resident, and joins a vote or
review process that the object's own governing law accepts. Adoption changes a
pinned method reference; it does not rewrite old history or grant ambient rights.

This construction makes Full OO, docuverse, private work and collective choice
part of normal use. It can support a story universe, a technical instrument, an
informal community plan or a shared workshop without pretending they all share
one taste or institution. Fictional roles remain distinct from real principals;
an in-world narrator cannot authorize real payments just by narrating them.

Use the exact Bend owner's contract, R/designs/BEND-WORLD-LANGUAGE-20261003.txt:
* Existing generic executable Program ID stays intact. Source artifact sidecar
  binds source/import lock, elaborated Book, semantics, codecs, Plan ABI and
  actual provenance. A source link is not a compiler refinement proof.
* Kernel-created sample -> pure typed World.Plan: complete dependencies, exact
  effects/messages, independent committed private return slots, canonical charge.
* Receiver checks current grants/revocation/law, source/kind roots, resource and
  release policy. Typechecking, compiled native success and a backend attestation
  cannot create rights. Effects do not fire during speculative computation.
* Self/super and immutable method closure belong to OO semantics; a governance
  policy graph is different. Native Nat48 versus unbounded BendTT and exact
  charge must be qualified, not guessed from C/JS performance.

C. A resident and service commons
--------------------------------
A participant contributes a home/capacity offer; another rents bounded work for
a resident using a published Bend service contract. The task may curate sources,
help author a world, moderate an agreed workflow, maintain an object, or perform
a private allocation. A group can sponsor it without giving the host its own
authority. The home exposes transcript/context, budget, outstanding work and
current permitted actions. The resident is a continuing participant, not merely
a one-shot background job. Sleep, reconnect, recovery and eventually a consented
move preserve exact retained work and audience boundaries.

Service exchange should price a specified service, not a generic numeric-job
market accidentally imported from Hellas. A quote binds method/input/source,
result predicate or human acceptance role, offer/capacity, deadline, fee and
recovery/release obligations. The deterministic Bend plan can be re-executed;
an external Hermes response has provider provenance and contract-defined
acceptance, not a theorem that its prose is true. Dispute/challenge mechanisms
apply only where an individually able, funded challenger and a decisive predicate
exist. Staking on a subjective answer does not make the answer factual.

Reuse agent-platform's actual lifecycle and renter-acknowledgment ideas, adapt to
Mini service owner contracts. B/agent-platform/src/lib.rs:1495-1569 checks pinned
renter signature, genuine history prefix and nonregression. That is stronger
than a host-signed log alone. It is not availability or complete execution proof.
SessionCarrier:185-204 retains a host-held signing seed; exporting it raw would
export custody. A movable home needs rekey/sealed successor handoff, old-host
fencing, acknowledged prefix and recovery obligations. share's implicit owner
Admin cannot override Mini's deliberately possible management lockout.

Use existing Mini resident request/completion/cancel/provider-custody machinery,
not a new agent scheduler per app. The shared activity index names exact source,
request, reserve, external dispatch, retained output, installed effects, release
and delivery. It does not make them one boolean. A pending provider dispatch
after a lost reply is located/recovered before creating new paid work. A result
can be retained while its audience has since lost permission; custody and release
are different decisions. A notice is a pointer to truth, not truth itself.

Economics must reserve actual disjoint capacity, not just have balances. Distinct
costs: canonical evaluator work, external service fees, preprocessing, cover
traffic, retained artifacts, storage repair, agreement and post-crash completion.
Reserve recovery service independently of new ordinary work. Provider quotes
may be compared using exact common dimensions; a low price with no fulfillable
capacity is not a valid allocation. Market fairness and fair physical scheduling
are separate obligations. Private branch-dependent rebates can leak information.

Receiving demonstration: a group sponsors a resident to review the studio's
pending change or run an allocation, sees source-scoped partial/terminal work,
cuts an unnecessary grant, loses the final reply, restarts and obtains the same
result without a second charge or dispatch. The granted publish destination
accepts only the exact authorized effect. Renter acknowledges history. Host
movement is a later real custody test, not a UI 'export' button promise.

Common implementation contract and economy of code
=================================================
The public API should not contain MarketAuthority, PollAuthority, DesktopAuthority
and AgentAuthority as four independent sources of truth. Domain modules express
their state machines and rules. Common Mini machinery supplies authenticated
samples, source binding, invocation/retry identity, dependency/current-law guards,
exact effect admission, agreement/reservations, custody and authorized release.
Domain adapters turn external evidence into exactly typed inputs; they cannot
select weaker verifier policy from their own uploaded bundle.

Keep a single generated family of source schemas/codecs/method intent forms/view
descriptors where it removes duplicate meaning. Share archive canonicalization,
manifest checking, bounded codecs and fsync mechanics, but keep typed snapshot
contracts: document cursor, app /var, resident continuation, private-share
generation and rehearsal are not interchangeable backups. A shared activity
index links each real phase machine instead of inventing a universal one.

Evidence-aware presentation, aligned with graded_execution_architecture:
Show concise promise cards with inspectable details for (1) exact module and
arithmetic/mechanism, (2) intended/selected execution plan, (3) observer-specific
disclosures, (4) correctness/privacy/availability trust assumptions separately,
(5) evidence actually received, (6) compute/reserve/install/release/delivery
phase, (7) costs and release budget. Native re-execution, proof, signatures,
attestation, community endorsement and optimizer certificate establish different
propositions. A plan can combine FHE, MPC, ZK and agreement. Avoid one ordered
mode ladder or a green 'verified/dark' flag. Unavailable plans refuse or await an
explicit authorized alternative, never silently expose private inputs.

Cross-system interoperability:
Keep native semantics transport-neutral. Browser extension, native app, CLI,
hosted app, fn or chat adapters carry the same exact request/result. Extension
ceremony participation requires actual separate device custody and current
opening authorization, not just signing the coordinator's summary. An app
package signature establishes package origin/bytes, not every action's authority.
External settlement requires exact resource identity/amount/recipient, finality,
custody and replay binding. B/intent/src/drex_routing.rs:1-61 is valuable dataflow
but explicitly uses mock proof acceptance/trusted mirror and one-vault fixture;
do not call it cross-chain atomicity. This architecture leaves an interchain
module boundary open without making external financial deployment October13.
