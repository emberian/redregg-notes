2. Two compilation products from one Bend live meaning.

A. Universal machine trace:
  fixed transition schema, varying exact book/program and input; private or
  public program choice is explicit. Bind exact pinned kernel and evaluator
  profile, code/book commitment, entry, initial/final data encoding, quota,
  outcome, exact step charge, typed ordered effects and native dependency trace.
  An accepted trace must force every transition, not merely contain a producer
  success bit. Public ROM fits a fixed trace relation; private program requires
  authenticated secret code access under the same trace constraints.

B. Fixed-program specialization:
  validate/elaborate the fixed exact BendTT book/entry, partially evaluate only
  public syntax, specialize the shared transition IR or compile the represented
  data fragment, emit one reusable descriptor/network for all inputs in declared
  shapes. Erasure removes dead types/annotations/motives/Q0 arguments and fields;
  it must preserve every live case, capture, label and quantity behavior. Symbolic
  branches become muxes, never specialization on secret data. General recursion
  is unrolled to a public cap or lowered through the bounded shared machine;
  termination Acc supplies no uniform finite circuit shape. Closures require
  explicit env/capture encoding; Q2 copies normalized Data and may share immutable
  heap nodes, it does not authorize duplication of closures or opaque calls.

Both products need a source-to-machine observation simulation, a machine-to-
constraint arbitrary-witness forcing theorem, and backend-specific consumer
refinement. There is no default Bend -> Nock translation; Nock registry and ABI
remain their own compatibility entry. Checker WNF is lazy and not the live Eval/
Walk CBV semantics. Upstream comp.ts erases Rwt evidence and unused live lets,
so native fast-path refinement is final-observation simulation rather than
literal equality of source step counts.

3. Public/private and shape are independent of source quantities.

Public receiving statement: source/kernel/compiler/profile IDs, chosen encoding
and arithmetic domain, commitments to any hidden program/data/initial state,
capability/policy revision context, effect/dependency commitments, declared caps,
and permitted final observation/release. Private: selected program bytes (when
that mode is supported), input/captures/heap/PC/control path/intermediate values,
private outputs. Q0/Q1/Q2 describe erasure/usage/Data copying, not visibility.

Caps bind code/book size, label dictionary, node count, closure/env frames, heap
allocation, cell/slot working set, recursion/fuel, exact input/output lengths,
integer limbs and effect count. Padded circuits/networks perform the whole public
schedule; halt/crash/exhaustion/overflow are distinct private result tags.
Overflow refuses or follows a predeclared resize/retry protocol; no take/truncate,
unknown label=>false, malformed=>zero or exhausted=>crash substitution.
The same profile owns charging. A specialized circuit may have different physical
work from source Eval: bind semantic charge and backend cost separately, prove
conversion, and never price merely the faster path while accepting a different
trace. Program-cache key includes the exact compiled source/book closure,
kernel pin, compiler and evaluator versions, encoding, public split, arithmetic
profile, all caps and backend/version, plus emitted bytes. Private source caches
must avoid exposing secret source identifiers publicly.

4. Arithmetic/backend distinctions.

Kernel Nat is Sigma/labels, not U32. comp.ts native U32 wraps and divides by zero
as zero; native Nat optimization has a bounded representation. Primitive names
cannot alone authorize replacing exact source definitions. Require definition
identity, a representation theorem, and overflow semantics. Scalar field equality
is exact only on an injective declared image. Integer addition/multiplication
requires range/carry/limb proofs; casting a 256-bit input to one BabyBear felt is
lossy. Reuse Mini AirRange/AirBignum/order gadgets when their proved domain fits.

A field add/mul descriptor can feed multiple physical backends, but their proof
obligations differ. MPC needs authenticated secret sharing and malicious circuit
execution/release; ZK needs a sound commitment/opening/transcript/proof consumer
for the bound trace; FHE needs matching plaintext ring, injective encoding or
explicit modular source semantics, evaluated ciphertext noise budget and exact
threshold opening. Field inverses/zero tests or branching do not become free
homomorphic primitives. Bread's bounded exact zero conversion costs B-1 products,
whole-field depth lower bound is >=20, and its B=8 measured multiplied-ciphertext
noise refusal is not a certified FHE security/noise theorem. General graded
modalities must preserve these separate domain/admission/release contracts.
